# Irish Eyes — V4 Rebuild Directive

V3 remains a valid good-looking draft and proof of the base visual language. V4 is an expansion/rebuild, not a repudiation of V3.

## User directives now binding

- No storyboard or asset previews in chat unless explicitly requested.
- Keep the entry shoreline footage with the boy.
- Remove the busy beach / beach-with-people / high-rise crowd material from the movie.
- `VID_20230413_171637512.mp4` may be used only as atmospheric ocean/sky/surf/dune material unless a later directive says otherwise; do not feature the visible beachgoers.
- Brandi's 2017 footage remains the real identity anchor, but it must not be the only meaningful media in the final film.
- V4 must reuse the effects already created and archived in this repository rather than inventing a separate unrelated effect language.

## Reusable effect stack to use visibly

From `general/reusable/irish-eyes-tools/` and `general/reusable/depth-parallax-25d/`:

- cinematic restoration;
- audio-reactive water shimmer;
- warm/cool halation and bloom;
- continuous depth-field 2.5D parallax;
- subject-protected camera travel.

From `general/reusable/silver-coin-tools/video_fx/`, adapted to Irish Eyes photorealism:

- volumetric light shafts / haze;
- lightning / wet-reflection modulation;
- camera breathing;
- noise/pigment-style dream dissolves used as optical transitions, not painterly conversion;
- motion-signature-aware temporal effects;
- temporal QC.

Additional approved visible optical effects already established during Irish Eyes development:

- prism / chromatic edge split;
- restrained motion echo trails;
- liquid/reflection distortion;
- dreamy refraction transitions.

## Visibility rule for V4

The effects may remain cinematic, but they cannot be so conservative that a normal viewer would reasonably say no effects were used. Major effect passages must be clearly visible in the exported movie and still preserve photographic identity and scene coherence.

## Media-balance rule

V4 must materially reduce the amount of repeated Brandi-source footage compared with V3. New moving material should include:

- boy shoreline storm passage;
- authored 2.5D Brandi memory passages;
- ocean/storm atmospheric passages derived from real B-roll;
- transition/effect sequences created from the established source world;
- other purpose-built photoreal moving media as needed.

## Full-export QC rule

No V4 review file may be presented to the user until the actual exported file has been scanned across the full runtime for:

- duration / codec / audio correctness;
- black frames / broken segments;
- long freeze runs;
- crowd/high-rise beach recurrence;
- actual presence of authored-media sections;
- actual visible presence of planned effects;
- excessive repetition of the 2017 source;
- identity continuity;
- transition failures;
- overall visual coherence.

A contact sheet or representative-frame check alone is not sufficient to call the movie finished.
