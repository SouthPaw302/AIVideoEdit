## Acceptance change

A render is not ready merely because codec, duration, black-frame, and freeze checks pass. Before user delivery, the final exported file must also receive a full-runtime content review confirming:

- intended authored-media sequences are actually present;
- effects are visibly present at intended strength;
- excluded footage is absent;
- media diversity matches the intended movie structure;
- repeated source material is intentional rather than filler;
- scene transitions and identity continuity remain clean.
