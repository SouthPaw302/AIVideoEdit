## Purpose

Prove that the Magic Gate can also be crossed quietly. This is intended for the final emotional return, not as another spectacle shot.

## V1

V1 used real source motion, protected subject separation, independent water/sky motion and restrained dolly-zoom perception. It rendered cleanly but was rejected because the subject remained too underexposed and the final pullback produced a visible lower-right border artifact.

## V2 method

- real source motion slowed across the end window;
- subject mask derived from the real frame;
- clean-ish source-derived background plate created by inpainting only behind the protected subject;
- background compression/release while the real subject subtly grows: restrained dolly-zoom/time-dilation perception;
- independent source-derived water movement;
- faint source-derived cloud drift;
- stronger but localized subject shadow lift;
- motivated warm sunlight/edge light along the subject boundary;
- halation and conservative finishing;
- artifact-producing final shrink removed.

No replacement person or generated scene is used.

## Magic Gate

**KEEP — QUIET PROVISIONAL PASS.**

This shot is deliberately subtler than Spatial Entry or Reflection Portal. Its success condition is not an obvious portal or storm event; it is the sensation that the world breathes and changes depth around a still-recognizable real human moment. V2 removes the visible edge artifact and improves the silhouette readability while retaining the backlit character of the source.

## Reusable lesson

The Magic Gate does not require spectacle. A closing shot may pass through controlled differential depth, protected real motion, light recovery and environmental breathing when those treatments alter perception without announcing themselves as an effect.
