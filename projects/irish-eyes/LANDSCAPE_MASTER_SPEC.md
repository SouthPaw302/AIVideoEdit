# Irish Eyes — Landscape Master Specification

Branch: `song/irish-eyes`

## Locked aspect rule

The finished Irish Eyes music film and its YouTube packaging are **16:9 landscape only**.

Vertical/portrait renders created during the effect-laboratory phase are reference/proof assets only. They are not valid final-film masters.

## Source reality

`Brandi South Florida 2017.mp4` is encoded as 1280x720 but carries a -90 degree display matrix. When displayed correctly, the camera capture is portrait-oriented. Therefore disabling rotation produces a sideways picture and is not a valid landscape solution.

## Correct landscape treatment

Portrait source is converted to cinematic 16:9 through authored reframing rather than pillarboxing:

- use real footage for horizontal close-ups, medium crops, motion fragments and detail shots;
- use shot-specific crop tracking so faces, hair, hands, horizon and water remain compositionally intentional;
- use source-derived depth/environment extension where a wider frame is needed;
- use landscape environmental memory assets for road/window/lake/world-expansion shots;
- use generated or external support material only where it extends the established world and passes Magic Gate QC;
- never use blurred vertical-video sidebars or generic phone-video pillarbox treatment in the finished film.

## Working format

- aspect: 16:9
- current landscape working proof: 1280x720
- current cadence: 30 fps to preserve source motion
- final delivery target may be upscaled after picture lock and finishing; do not change cadence casually before final QC
- color target: Rec.709 cinematic grade
- audio target: 48 kHz stereo when remastered song master is mounted

## Landscape continuity proof

A 16:9 continuity proof has been created from the approved/provisional visual families:

- `landscape_16x9/continuity/IRISH_EYES_LANDSCAPE_CONTINUITY_REEL_V4_16x9.mp4`
- 1280x720
- 30 fps
- approximately 55.47 s

This reel is a reframing/continuity validation asset, not a final song edit. Final individual shots should be rebuilt from the highest-quality source material in landscape rather than simply cropping low-resolution portrait proofs where quality would suffer.

## Final-film rule

Before song-timed rough assembly, every selected shot must be assigned one of these landscape strategies:

1. real 16:9 crop from portrait source;
2. subject + source-derived landscape extension;
3. landscape environmental memory shot;
4. landscape transition/portal shot;
5. generated/support landscape shot that preserves the established visual world.

No final master may contain an accidental portrait canvas.
