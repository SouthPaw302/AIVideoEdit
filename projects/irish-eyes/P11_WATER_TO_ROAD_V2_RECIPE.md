# Irish Eyes — P11 Water → Wet Road Memory V2

Status: render recipe locked; V1 rejected from the movie.

## Why V1 failed

The first proof successfully changed category from water toward a road, but the twin warm reflection streaks read like procedural/fake headlights painted over water. The transformation was technically clean but not premium enough for the Magic Gate.

## V2 visual law

The road must appear because **perception changes**, not because a road graphic is drawn over the footage.

1. Start from the real frame-520 South Florida water/sky plate.
2. Preserve the real sky and horizon as long as possible.
3. Push the camera toward the lower water/reflection field.
4. Compress the horizontal water ripple texture into a perspective trapezoid converging on the real horizon.
5. Darken/desaturate that transformed real-water texture until it reads as wet asphalt.
6. Turn the source sun reflection into one narrow neutral/warm wet center sheen — no orange headlight blobs.
7. If lane information appears, use only extremely faint broken reflected-sky glints near the edges; no bright painted road markings.
8. Use horizon mist/haze to hide the exact moment the material category changes.
9. Continue forward motion after the road is established so the viewer feels the camera has entered the memory world.
10. Keep the entire effect source-derived; no generated person and no generic AI-video road.

## Magic Gate criteria

KEEP only if:
- the opening unmistakably begins as the real South Florida water;
- the ending unmistakably reads as a wet road at dusk/night;
- there is no single frame where the road looks like a flat overlay;
- no fake headlight blobs or obvious drawn road graphics appear;
- the transformation feels dreamlike but physically continuous;
- black/freeze QC is clean.

If V2 still looks procedural, abandon this particular transformation and build P12 from real/photographic road material rather than forcing the water texture farther than it can support.
