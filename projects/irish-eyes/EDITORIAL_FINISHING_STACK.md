# Irish Eyes — Living-Frame + Editorial Finishing Stack

Branch: `song/irish-eyes`

This is binding production direction for the finished film.

## 1. Build living cinematic assets first

Selected source stills are not finished shots. Each strong frame should be turned into a short cinematic asset using only the effects that serve that image.

Approved effect families include:

1. Gaussian spatial treatment / actual 3DGS where source coverage supports it;
2. hybrid NeRF volumetric atmosphere/light where useful;
3. continuous soft-depth 2.5D parallax;
4. living-image micro-motion: hair, dress, foliage, body sway, clouds;
5. water/reflection systems: shimmer, ripple, sun streaks, shoreline distortion;
6. volumetric weather: storm haze, rain, mist, fog, cloud shadow, lightning;
7. optical treatment: prism, refraction, halation, bloom, glints;
8. temporal memory echoes used selectively and without identity ghosting;
9. authored transitions using reflections, water, sun, fog, objects, depth, or optical matches;
10. music-directed modulation driven by measured song energy/transients.

Effects are assigned artistically per frame. Do not stack all ten blindly.

## 2. Shot-package output

Each selected hero frame should become a recoverable shot package containing, where useful:

- original source frame/window;
- protected photographic subject plate;
- alpha/mattes;
- clean or extended background;
- depth/layer separation;
- water/sky/reflection/atmosphere elements;
- generated support media when the real source cannot supply the needed environment;
- effect assets/presets;
- transition entry/exit material;
- short rendered moving proof;
- QC decision and notes.

The short moving proof is the actual unit used to judge whether the still has become a convincing cinematic scene.

## 3. Assemble the music film only from approved assets

Once enough living-shot assets exist, assemble the full song edit with deliberate rhythm, recurring motifs, contrast, rests, and returns to photographic reality.

The finished sequence must feel like a directed film, not a collection of effect demonstrations.

## 4. Full professional video-editor finishing pass happens AFTER picture assembly

The assembled movie is not final. Run a dedicated finishing pass across the complete export.

### Color correction

- normalize exposure shot-to-shot;
- recover usable shadow/highlight detail;
- correct white balance and unwanted casts;
- match skin tone and the coral/red dress consistently;
- match generated/support material to the real source;
- repair abrupt brightness or color jumps between shots.

### Creative grade

- establish coherent South Florida sun/water color language;
- refine contrast and black level;
- refine saturation/vibrance without clipping skin or dress color;
- selectively strengthen warm sunset light and cool storm/water separation;
- use secondary/local adjustments for sky, water, skin, dress, foliage, highlights and reflections;
- preserve natural photographic texture.

### Highlight / optical finishing

- control bloom and halation after final exposure is established;
- protect bright sun/reflection detail from ugly clipping;
- refine lens/prism/refraction intensity so it feels motivated;
- preserve subtle specular glints and water sparkle through the final grade.

### Detail / texture finishing

- restrained denoise only when needed;
- natural sharpening/detail recovery after scaling/encoding decisions;
- grain/texture matching between real, generated, 2.5D, NeRF and 3DGS-derived material;
- remove waxy skin, halos, ringing, edge chatter and oversharpening.

### Stabilization / motion consistency

- correct unintended jitter while retaining intentional camera energy;
- check optical-flow/interpolation artifacts;
- check loop seams and repeated-motion tells;
- ensure parallax/volumetric/particle movement remains temporal and coherent after assembly.

### Editorial finishing

- inspect every cut and transition in context;
- tighten any dead section;
- remove repeated visual information unless it is an intentional motif;
- protect strong musical accents and lyrical beats;
- verify no transition creates identity double exposure or accidental black/flash frames.

### Delivery / final QC

Scan the actual final encoded movie, not just the timeline or intermediate assets.

Verify:

- color/saturation/contrast survived export;
- effects remain visibly present;
- no crushed blacks or clipped channels;
- no frozen or duplicate passages;
- no black/flash frames;
- no broken masks, hair edges or identity drift;
- no missing/incorrect footage;
- no obvious seams or compression damage;
- correct duration, frame rate, resolution, codec and audio sync.

## 5. Production principle

The sequence is:

**real source → selected still/window → living cinematic scene → approved asset library → full music-film edit → professional correction/grade/finishing → final-export QC**

Do not stop after animating frames. Do not stop after assembling the timeline. The finishing pass is part of the creative work and is required before Irish Eyes is considered complete.
