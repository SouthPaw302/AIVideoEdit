# Irish Eyes — Visual DNA

Branch: `song/irish-eyes`

## Source anchors

- Primary real-footage source: `Brandi South Florida 2017.mp4`
- Music master: `Irish eyes (Remastered).wav`
- Source footage is the identity anchor. Brandi's face, body, clothing, location, lighting direction, and South Florida waterfront atmosphere must remain recognizable and photorealistic.
- Do not replace the real subject with a generated lookalike. Generated frames/effects must extend or enhance the source rather than overwrite her identity.

## Selected visual language

### Primary

1. **Photoreal narrative** — the finished piece should feel like a memory captured by a real camera, not AI illustration.
2. **Stock-footage / documentary realism** — preserve the intimate, found-footage quality of the 2017 South Florida source.
3. **Cinematic realism** — improve framing, grading, lens behavior, pacing, depth, and light while retaining photographic truth.
4. **Naturalism** — physically believable skin, water, clouds, hair, wind, fabric, reflections, and camera movement.

### Secondary / controlled enhancements

5. **Cinematic living-image treatment** — selected extracted frames may be held and extended with natural micro-motion: hair, dress fabric, water, cloud drift, breathing-scale motion, reflection movement, and subtle camera travel.
6. **2.5D parallax scene graphs** — use depth-separated frames for slow dimensional moves when the original clip cannot provide enough coverage.
7. **Selective 3D Gaussian Splatting / NeRF environment reconstruction** — only for waterfront/background spatial extensions where the available frames provide enough parallax. Keep Brandi herself in the original photographic plate or carefully composited masks; do not reconstruct her face/body as a splat/NeRF character.
8. **Integrated audio-reactive cinematography** — musical energy should modulate light bloom, water shimmer, atmospheric depth, micro-speed ramps, edit density, and subtle camera accents. Avoid obvious visualizer overlays.

## Transition language

- **Continuous dream journey** as the dominant transition logic: the film should feel like a remembered afternoon stretching across the entire song.
- **Recursive dream transitions** used sparingly through real visual motifs already present in the footage: sun glare, water reflections, clouds, sunglasses/eye-line, dress movement, and horizon light.
- Avoid synthetic morphing of the subject's face or body.

## Effect philosophy

The effects must remain invisible enough that a viewer can believe the footage was captured cinematically in-camera:

- restrained filmic grade and highlight rolloff
- optical bloom/halation around the low sun
- stabilized or intentionally floated camera motion
- selective slow motion / optical-flow retiming where it survives QC
- clean frame interpolation only when necessary
- controlled depth-of-field enhancement
- water reflection and cloud motion extensions
- subtle light leaks/refraction only when motivated by the sun/water
- re-framing and portrait crops from high-quality extracted frames
- natural grain only if needed to unify generated extensions with the 2017 footage

## Explicit exclusions

Do not use painterly animation, graphic-novel treatment, collage, cut-paper, anime, plastic CGI, fantasy illustration, aggressive plasma/tunnel visualizers, or obvious AI face morphing. Abstract passages may appear only as extremely brief optical/reflection phenomena derived from the real scene.

## Ending logic

Resolve back into the real South Florida image and hold on a believable human moment rather than ending on an effects showcase. The final emotional impression should be memory, warmth, distance, and recognition.
