## 1. Governing creative intent

This is a long-form YouTube music film, not a visualizer, slideshow, generic AI-video montage, or one source clip with invisible polish.

The visual philosophy is hybrid source-derived cinema:

- real Brandi footage and real extracted frames are the emotional/identity anchor;
- real photography and real-world environmental plates are preferred where possible;
- 2.5D, depth separation, optical flow, temporal motion, compositing, shaders, water/reflection systems, volumetrics, prism/halation, camera/perception moves and real-time spatial methods are encouraged;
- selective generated support material is allowed for surreal extensions, transitions, environments and missing visual beats when it genuinely improves the film;
- generated material must not replace Brandi's identity or turn the whole production into generic AI-rendered video;
- failed generated support that changes the person/location/style is rejected;
- every effect must survive rendered QC and must be artistically visible in the finished film.

The user explicitly wants zooming, altered perception and the feeling that still frames/worlds can open up and become explorable cinematic spaces.

## 2. Final-format law — 16:9 landscape only

The finished Irish Eyes film and YouTube packaging are **16:9 landscape**.

Working/final picture canvas:

- 1280x720
- 30 fps
- 16:9

The source MP4 is encoded 1280x720 with a `-90°` display matrix. The correct displayed capture is portrait. Disabling rotation merely makes Brandi sideways and is not a landscape solution.

Landscape production therefore uses authored horizontal reframing:

- real-source closeups and medium crops;
- subject/environment separation;
- source-derived background extension;
- 2.5D/environmental reconstruction where appropriate;
- true widescreen environmental memory shots;
- no blurred vertical-video sidebars;
- no pillarboxed phone-video treatment;
- portrait effect-lab renders are reference/proof assets only and are not final masters.

## 7. Literal 3D Gaussian Splatting decision

The source was tested for true 3DGS viability.

Result: stable waterfront background is mostly explained by planar/global camera motion with roughly 1–2 px residual at proof resolution. This clip does not provide strong enough multiview baseline to justify a forced splat training pass.

Decision:

- do **not** force true 3DGS from this source clip;
- reserve literal 3DGS/SuperSplat for footage with deliberate camera translation/arc/multiview capture;
- continue using source-derived depth/perception methods here;
- Gaussian-shaped optical/light fields are still allowed but must not be called 3DGS.

Read: `projects/irish-eyes/3DGS_VIABILITY_2026-09-04.md`.
