# Irish Eyes — User Directives

These directives override presentation habits and production defaults.

1. **Do not create additional storyboards.** The project already has sufficient storyboard/concept material.
2. **Irish Eyes is source-first hybrid cinema, not a fully AI-generated video.** Real footage and extracted frames remain the visual truth and emotional anchor, but selectively generated material is allowed when it serves the film.
3. **Real footage and real extracted frames are the primary identity-bearing material.** `Brandi South Florida 2017.mp4` and its frame set remain the canonical source for Brandi and the South Florida reality layer.
4. **Make existing material come alive before replacing it.** Approved approaches include real-motion retiming and looping, optical flow/interpolation, masking/rotoscoping, alpha plates, compositing, 2.5D depth separation, Structure-from-Motion, photogrammetry, 3D Gaussian Splatting, source-trained reconstruction, conventional/procedural VFX, particles, water/reflection systems, refraction, halation, bloom, volumetrics, camera moves, zoom/perception effects, color grading and editorial finishing.
5. **3D Gaussian Splatting is specifically encouraged when the source provides enough real viewpoint/parallax coverage.** Reconstruct the real waterfront/environment from real source frames, then navigate/render it with a real-time splat renderer such as PlayCanvas/SuperSplat. Do not reconstruct Brandi as a synthetic identity-bearing splat; protect/mask the moving human subject and composite her separately where required.
6. **Generative imagery/video may be used selectively as support material.** Good uses include environmental extensions, surreal memory spaces, impossible transitions, reflections, atmosphere, dream fragments, texture/plate creation, clone/surreal variants derived from a real frame, and missing visual beats that cannot be recovered convincingly from the source.
7. **Generated content must not replace Brandi's recognizable identity or turn the full project into generic AI video.** No synthetic Brandi lookalikes, face replacement, anatomy drift, waxy identity reconstruction, or wholesale replacement of real-world anchor scenes.
8. **When source coverage is insufficient for a real reconstruction, choose deliberately between strong 2.5D, source-derived compositing, procedural treatment, or a clearly intentional generated support element.** Do not force bad geometry and do not pretend a generated scene is source reconstruction.
9. **Procedural effects are allowed and encouraged.** Shader-driven fog, rain, mist, light shafts, reflection distortion, particles, lens effects, chromatic/prism behavior and similar VFX may combine with both real and selectively generated support layers.
10. **Generated assets must have a concrete production purpose.** They may become a shot, environment extension, transition plate, surreal insert, texture, matte, reflection, atmospheric element, or compositing layer. Do not create concept art for its own sake.
11. **Prioritize rendered moving footage.** Work should advance through enhanced real footage, source-derived loops, 2.5D shots, real 3D reconstruction, PlayCanvas/SuperSplat camera moves, selective generated support, optical transitions, compositing, scene assembly and final video renders.
12. Keep work in the current chat/project workflow. Do not move production to ChatGPT Work unless the user explicitly requests it.
13. Ask for more real user footage only when a specific missing real-world shot or additional parallax coverage would materially improve the finished film.
14. **Do not preview internal frame/contact-sheet assets in chat unless the user explicitly asks.** Internal QC is fine.

## Governing visual philosophy

The goal is not to make a generic AI-generated music video. The goal is to build a cinematic hybrid around real captured imagery: real footage carries identity, truth and continuity; reconstruction and VFX open that footage into space; selective generative elements add surrealism, missing environments, impossible transitions and visual magic where they genuinely strengthen the story.

The final picture may move fluidly between real, reconstructed, procedural and generated layers, but it should always feel authored as one film and repeatedly return to recognizable photographic reality.
