# Irish Eyes — Support Plates 01

Branch: `song/irish-eyes`

Purpose: source real/licensed environmental stills for Act II / Act III memory-world coverage when the 31-second Brandi source does not contain the required road/house/lake environment. These are support plates only. Motion, parallax, rain, reflections, camera moves, compositing, transitions and finishing remain authored inside the Irish Eyes production stack.

## Governing rule

- Brandi remains real-source-only unless the user explicitly approves otherwise.
- Do not use AI-video generation to replace the movie.
- Support stills may be real licensed photography, source-derived texture, or tightly controlled generated environment material.
- Reject any generated plate that drifts to a new person/location/style when the shot requires continuity.
- Prefer CC0/public-domain photography when a real plate exists.
- Preserve source/license metadata in the shot package.

## P11 Water → Road status

P11 V1/V2/V3/V4 remain revision lineage only.

- V1: fake reflected-light streaks read like procedural headlights.
- V2: perspective fold read as a dark geometric wedge.
- V3: explicit road geometry / dashed centerline looked constructed.
- V4: removed lane markings and hard trapezoid, retained source-derived wet texture and soft roadside falloff, but final surface remained too murky to convincingly read as road.

Decision: do not promote any P11 version yet.

Next route: transition from real South Florida water into a real licensed road/rain plate using reflection geometry, optical haze and refractive handoff rather than trying to synthesize asphalt purely from the waterfront pixels.
