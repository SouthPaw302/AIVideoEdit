## Story premise

The film is a photoreal memory journey built around the song's lyrical anchors: **Irish eyes**, **Spanish hair**, the recurring **sigh no more** refrain, memory, home, ancestry, love, distance, and the horizon. Brandi is the recurring human anchor. The South Florida waterfront is the present-day reality layer. New imagery and motion may expand into memory, ancestry, roads, old houses, rain, lake water, candlelight, and nocturnal landscapes, but the film repeatedly returns to the real 2017 footage so the audience never loses the emotional truth of the source.

The verbatim lyric sheet is not currently stored on this branch. Until it is recovered, scene timing follows measured musical structure plus known lyric motifs; exact line-level sync will be refined without changing the movie's established visual arc.

## Shot-source hierarchy

For every scene, use this priority order:

1. real source video motion;
2. real source frames rebuilt into loops, slow motion, or living images;
3. real-source-derived 2.5D / environmental extension;
4. photoreal generated support footage/images based on the established world;
5. abstract optical material only when derived from water/light/reflection.

## Additional real-video request rule

Do not interrupt production merely because a generated scene could be improved with more footage. Continue using the strongest available method. Ask the user for more real videos only when a specific missing real-world scene or identity-critical shot would materially improve the finished film, and describe exactly what footage would help.
