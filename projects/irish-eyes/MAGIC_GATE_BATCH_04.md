Decision: **REJECT.**

A distant translucent full-body duplicate read as a pasted ghost in the sky rather than refraction or memory.

### V2

Decision: **REJECT.**

Confining the duplicate to the water fixed the floating-ghost problem, but the memory image became too subtle to read at normal playback.

Decision: **REJECT / preserve concept only.**

An upside-down, more visible underwater/refracted duplicate was readable but still felt like an apparition/shadow rather than a premium physically motivated reflection effect.

## Current lesson

The strongest Irish Eyes magic continues to come from **perceptual transitions with a physically motivated destination**:

- move into real water;
- move past the real subject into the waterfront;
- enter the story through real sunglasses;
- return through the same light/reflection logic.

Literal ghost-clone overlays are weaker and should not be used merely because they look surreal.
