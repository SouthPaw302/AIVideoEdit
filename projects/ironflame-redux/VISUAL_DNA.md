# IronFlameRedux Visual DNA

Status: **visual direction locked from current-production storyboard; timing lock pending audio master.**

## Core identity
- Protagonist: a single recurring female IronFlame; dark wavy/curly hair with braided details, weathered/soot-marked face, strong cheekbones, resolute expression, dark leather/metal accents, practical mythic clothing rather than glossy fantasy armor.
- Identity rule: once a clean identity anchor is approved, preserve exact facial identity and age across every human-bearing shot. No shot-to-shot face regeneration drift.
- Essence: forging, loss, memory, oath, endurance, rising.
- Emotional progression: haunted -> determined -> ascendant.

## World
Rugged medieval / dark-folk mythic naturalism. Fire is physical, dangerous, and sacred; iron is tactile and worked; ruins, mountains, halls, moonlit woods, ash, rain, and dawn feel inhabitable rather than theme-park fantasy.

Avoid generic portal/gate imagery and arbitrary magic-VFX spectacle. Symbolism should emerge from forge light, circular marks, flame, iron, wolves, landscape, architecture, smoke, and human action.

## Style blend
Primary: cinematic realism + mythic naturalism + dark fantasy restraint.
Secondary: painterly surface character and controlled animation/visualizer inserts when motivated by the music.

Target is a living-image film: authored depth, internal motion, atmosphere, light behavior, texture and camera. Never a slideshow, generic Ken Burns sequence, or unconstrained generative-video morphing.

## Palette journey
1. **Ember orange** — forge, blood warmth, ignition.
2. **Iron blue** — travel, moon, solitude, resolve.
3. **Ash gray** — cost, aftermath, memory.
4. **Dawn gold** — integration, legacy, continuation.

Black/dark navy grounds the entire film. Gold is earned as the story rises; it should not flatten every scene into orange.

## Motifs
Fire, iron, oath, wolves, journey, legacy, forge, scars/marks, smoke, embers, sparks, rain, fog, ash, dust.

## Camera language
- slow push-ins for resolve/intimacy;
- depth-aware drift/parallax for landscapes and architecture;
- restrained handheld energy for conflict/forge work;
- occasional Dutch tension only when narratively justified;
- rack-focus/lens-breathing behavior for memory/recognition;
- match cuts and motivated visual handoffs rather than generic crossfades.

## Motion language
- layered foreground/midground/background movement;
- cloth/hair motion that preserves body/face identity;
- fire, smoke, rain, embers, ash and fog as localized systems;
- practical-light breathing and moving shadow;
- audio-reactive intensity, not audio-reactive chaos.

## Audio-reactive intent from storyboard
- bass / low end -> fire intensity and glow;
- mids -> sparks / particle density;
- highs -> ember/highlight activity;
- vocals -> depth/focus emphasis;
- reverb / sustain -> fog/atmosphere density;
- transients -> cut/impact/camera accents.

Actual envelopes and timing must be derived from the Redux audio master once received.

## Technical target
Storyboard target: 16:9, 24 fps, cinematic Rec.709, 48 kHz audio, final-quality widescreen master. 4K is the design target; proof renders may be smaller but must preserve composition and visible FX.
