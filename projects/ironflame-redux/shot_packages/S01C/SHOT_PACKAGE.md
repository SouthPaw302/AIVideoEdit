## Source-frame brief
16:9 heroic close-up / tight three-quarter portrait of the same IronFlame heroine defined by the storyboard. Long dark wavy/curly hair with small braids and restrained metal/leather details; soot/dirt and lived-in marks; strong cheekbones; fierce but controlled expression; warm forge rim light across one side of the face with cool near-black shadow on the other. Embers and faint smoke exist around her but never obscure eyes or facial landmarks. No beauty-glam treatment, no glossy skin, no random face paint redesign, no text or UI elements.

This still becomes the identity reference for all later human-bearing generation. Once accepted, later scene generations should match this face, apparent age, hairline, eye spacing, nose, jaw and overall proportions.

## Layer / depth plan
- FG: sparse defocused embers / smoke wisps.
- MG: face/hair/shoulders; face mask protected from destructive warps.
- BG: soft forge light, dark workshop shapes, optional fire practical.
- Separate hair fringe and shoulder/cloth where useful for micro-motion.

## Motion grammar
- breath and tiny posture settling;
- hair fringe/loose strands moving subtly from forge air;
- eye focus and facial geometry remain fixed unless using a trusted source animation method that preserves exact identity;
- practical light breath and ember drift create life without face morphing;
- camera: slow physical push or depth-aware move, never crop-only zoom.

## Hard identity QC
Reject immediately for: face-shape drift, altered age, asymmetric eye corruption, new scars/marks between frames, wax/plastic skin, mouth warping, double exposure, duplicated jewelry, broken hairline, floating embers covering key facial landmarks, or matte halos.

## Proof acceptance
KEEP only if the shot feels alive while the woman's identity is effectively unchanged from first to last inspected frame. This proof becomes the visual identity anchor for the Redux production.
