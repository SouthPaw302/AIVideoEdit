# S09B — Rise Through Ruins Proof Package

Sequence: S09 RISE
Role: ash-gray -> dawn-gold transition, architectural depth and forward-motion benchmark.
Current source authority: `IFR-SRC-001` (`Ironflame: A Mythic Visual Storyboard.png`).
Timing: provisional until Redux audio master arrives.

## Source-frame brief
16:9 wide/medium rear three-quarter shot of the locked IronFlame heroine walking through a ruined cathedral/hall or ancient stone structure toward real dawn light. Architecture is weathered, grounded and traversable. Ash-gray shadows dominate the foreground while restrained dawn gold enters from ahead. Sparse embers/ash and cool fog reveal spatial depth. No literal portal, magic doorway, glowing dimensional gate or fantasy energy tunnel; the destination is physical daylight.

## Layer / depth plan
- FG: nearest broken stone, ash/dust, optional soft out-of-focus debris.
- MG-A: heroine figure/cloak/hair.
- MG-B: columns/arches/rubble on independent depth bands.
- BG: exterior dawn/horizon and distant architecture.
- Protect body silhouette and walking geometry from warping.

## Motion grammar
- authored slow forward camera drift plus heroine walking/settling motion from a trustworthy source method;
- fog/dust moves independently at different depth planes;
- dawn light grows gently as spatial relationship changes, not as a global exposure pump;
- cloth/hair react to space/wind without anatomy deformation.

## Spatial note
The scene strongly benefits from real depth separation. Current registered 2.5D/spatial adapters are `proof_required`; do not claim those IDs in production until their proof/promotion state permits it. Layered authored camera motion may be prototyped truthfully as a non-claimed shot method and documented as such.

## Artifact guard
Reject bent architecture, impossible perspective, sliding floor planes, silhouette doubling, identity drift, portal-like glow, global zoom-only movement, fog matte edges or looping ash patterns.

## Proof acceptance
KEEP only if the viewer feels genuine forward progress through physical space, the palette visibly evolves toward dawn without losing shadow detail, and the shot remains calm enough to read as rise/integration rather than another effects climax.
