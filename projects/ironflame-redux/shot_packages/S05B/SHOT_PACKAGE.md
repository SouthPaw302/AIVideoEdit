# S05B — Moon/Wolf Exterior Proof Package

Sequence: S05 WILD & FREE
Role: cool-palette exterior, animal/human continuity and quiet-motion benchmark.
Current source authority: `IFR-SRC-001` (`Ironflame: A Mythic Visual Storyboard.png`).
Timing: provisional until Redux audio master arrives.

## Source-frame brief
16:9 moonlit mythic-naturalist exterior. The locked IronFlame heroine shares frame with a real-looking dark wolf in a rugged forest/ridge environment under a large but natural moon. Iron-blue and ash-gray dominate; only tiny warm carryover accents remain on metal/skin. Wind, fog and distant weather create depth. The heroine is alert and free rather than posing like a fantasy pin-up. Wolf anatomy must be realistic; no glowing magical eyes unless physically motivated by reflection.

## Layer / depth plan
- FG: branches/grass/rain or fog wisps.
- MG-A: heroine profile/three-quarter figure, cloak/hair separated where possible.
- MG-B: wolf with clean silhouette and independent depth.
- BG: forest/ridge, moon/clouds, distant fog layers.

## Motion grammar
- independent slow fog/rain/branch motion;
- subtle fur, hair and cloak movement from the same wind field;
- heroine and wolf remain anatomically stable;
- camera: low-amplitude lateral drift/parallax or slow follow, no orbit gimmick;
- moon/cloud relationship must not slide unnaturally.

## Approved FX candidates
- `FX2-MOTION-002` localized_living_flow
- `FX2-ATM-001` advected_smoke for fog-like atmosphere when visually appropriate
- `FX2-ATM-002` rain_plane if weather is visible
- `FX2-LIGHT-002` moving_light_field at restrained intensity

## Artifact guard
Reject duplicated limbs/paws, wolf face morph, fused heroine/wolf silhouettes, impossible moon geometry, floating rain layer, global wobble, cloak-body separation, identity drift, or fake neon-blue grading.

## Proof acceptance
KEEP only if the shot feels physically windy/cold/alive, the heroine identity remains locked, the wolf remains believable, and depth is readable through atmosphere/occlusion rather than a flat zoom.
