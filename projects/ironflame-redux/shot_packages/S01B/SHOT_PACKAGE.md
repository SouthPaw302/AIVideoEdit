Sequence: S01 THE FORGE

Timing: provisional until Redux audio master arrives.

## Source-frame brief
16:9 cinematic medium shot of the locked IronFlame heroine actively working at a real forge. Dark wavy/curly hair with braided details, soot and sweat, practical dark leather/metal workwear, no glossy fantasy costume. Glowing iron on an anvil, hammer in motion, localized sparks and smoke, strong warm forge rim light against deep navy/black surroundings. Tactile iron, wood, ash and fire. No text, runic UI, portal, magic doorway or ornamental fantasy clutter.

The source still must be composed for animation: clear foreground sparks/forge edge, heroine and anvil in midground, dark workshop/background practicals behind her.

## Layer / depth plan
- FG: nearest sparks/embers, forge lip/tools, optional drifting smoke strand.
- MG-A: heroine body/arms/hair/hammer.
- MG-B: anvil and glowing billet.
- BG: workshop architecture, practical flames, smoke volume, distant ironwork.
- Separate masks for face/hands/hammer/iron/fire where feasible.

## Motion grammar
- real hammer/action impulse, not whole-frame wobble;
- hair/cloth micro-motion only where physically plausible;
- smoke and ember advection with independent depth;
- localized fire movement and practical-light response;
- camera: restrained handheld energy or subtle forward drift, not Ken Burns zoom.
