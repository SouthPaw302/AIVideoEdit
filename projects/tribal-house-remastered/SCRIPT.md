# Production Script — Tribal House Remastered

Target: 305.000 s @ 24 fps = 7,320 frames.

The script follows the locked 19-shot storyboard. Every shot remains inside the same mythic ritual world. Living-scene shots prioritize independent fire/smoke/ash/water/light motion and protect identity/topology. Cinematic shots are reserved for actual passage, convergence, or spatial progression that cannot be honestly represented by a held living plate.

## S01 — frames 0-239
Invocation I: Near-black volcanic ritual chamber; one low ember circle begins to breathe.

Mode: living_scene
Animation: Internal material/environment motion first; restrained depth/camera only after local motion is established.
Transition: motivated light/atmosphere/geometry handoff
Motion regions: embers, smoke, firelight, dust
Protected: central spirit seed, sacred geometry, architecture
