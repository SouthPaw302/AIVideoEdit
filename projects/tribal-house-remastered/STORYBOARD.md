# Storyboard — Tribal House Remastered

Locked visual story: an ancient fire-spirit is awakened by ritual percussion, gathers itself through earth, ash, bodies and sacred architecture, crosses thresholds as the beat intensifies, reaches full mythic manifestation, and exits transformed into calm power.

- **S01 00:00–00:10 — Invocation I** — Near-black volcanic ritual chamber; one low ember circle begins to breathe.
- **S02 00:10–00:20 — Invocation II** — Sacred floor geometry and vertical stone forms emerge as percussion gains body; the hidden force remains incomplete.
- **S03 00:20–00:30 — Invocation III** — The first coherent fire-spirit silhouette appears inside the ritual circle without fully revealing anatomy.
- **S04 00:30–00:44 — Gathering I** — Distant ritual figures and torch forms answer the awakened presence from the perimeter.
- **S05 00:44–00:58 — Gathering II** — Circular motion and dust pull inward toward the same central sacred space.
- **S06 00:58–01:22 — Manifestation I** — The spirit gains an identifiable head, torso and mantle of flame/feather-like energy while staying one coherent entity.
- **S07 01:22–01:46 — Manifestation II** — Wider reveal: the spirit stands inside monumental ruins as the environment begins responding to it.
- **S08 01:46–02:00 — Passage I** — The spirit advances through a stone threshold aligned with the percussion.
- **S09 02:00–02:14 — Passage II** — Volcanic shoreline or dark reflective basin carries the same spirit toward the next gate.
- **S10 02:14–02:35 — Peak Build I** — Fire, dust, cloth and reflected light become independently active around the spirit.
- **S11 02:35–02:56 — Peak Build II** — Ritual participants/environmental forms converge without obscuring the hero; impact rises.
- **S12 02:56–03:18 — Peak Build III** — Ceremonial overload: architecture glows along seams and atmosphere thickens while topology remains stable.
- **S13 03:18–03:33 — Threshold Crossing I** — The spirit enters a fire-and-water threshold; palette begins shifting from earthbound orange to luminous gold/indigo.
- **S14 03:33–03:48 — Threshold Crossing II** — The environment opens into a more mythic spatial plane; movement becomes less terrestrial.
- **S15 03:48–04:02 — Apotheosis I** — Monumental symmetrical reveal of the fully manifested spirit.
- **S16 04:02–04:16 — Apotheosis II** — The spirit rises or expands in presence while camera remains restrained and the world moves around it.
- **S17 04:16–04:33 — Release I** — Percussive aggression softens; fire and smoke movements become smoother and more fluid.
- **S18 04:33–04:50 — Release II** — The spirit recedes into a wide sacred landscape, power stabilized rather than extinguished.
- **S19 04:50–05:05 — Afterglow** — Ash, horizon glow and remaining embers settle into transformed stillness.
