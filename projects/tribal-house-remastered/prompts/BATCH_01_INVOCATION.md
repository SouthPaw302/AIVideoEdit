# Batch 01 — Invocation Generation Brief

Time: 00:00–00:30
Shots: S01, S02, S03

## Continuity anchor
One ancient fire-spirit begins as an almost invisible seed and becomes a coherent silhouette by S03. It is the same entity throughout. No unrelated phoenix cutaway, no different hero, no random masks.

## Image A — S01: Ember Seed
Near-black volcanic ritual chamber at night, immense stone space barely visible. A shallow sacred circular depression in the floor contains a tiny breathing ember core. Sparse ash suspended in darkness. Ancient stone geometry suggested, not fully revealed. No visible person yet. Painterly-cinematic realism, tactile mineral surfaces, black/charcoal/ember orange, strong negative space, 16:9.

## Image B — S02: Geometry Wakes
Same chamber and camera-world continuity. The ember core has grown. Thin firelit seams reveal circular sacred geometry and tall weathered stone forms around it. A not-yet-human shadow begins to form inside smoke above the circle. The figure must remain ambiguous and incomplete. No horror face. No generic neon EDM symbols. 16:9.

## Image C — S03: First Manifestation
Same sacred chamber. The central force now reads as one coherent mythic fire-spirit silhouette: elegant, powerful, mostly dark body/shape rimmed by ember/fire-feather energy, standing within the ritual circle. Anatomy is readable but not over-detailed. Monumental, ceremonial, calm power rather than aggression. Reflected fire glow on dark stone. 16:9.

## Image D — S03 alternate / coverage
Three-quarter wide angle of the same manifestation and same chamber, preserving silhouette language, floor circle, stone scale, and lighting direction. This is coverage, not a new location or new creature.
