# IronFlameNew QC

## Source ingest QC
- Master audio decodes correctly: PASS.
- Duration recorded: 244.68 s.
- Three reference MP4s decode correctly at 24 fps: PASS.
- Reference frame extraction/inspection completed: PASS.
- Portrait reference framing explicitly rejected as production framing; final is locked to 16:9 HD.

## Creative QC gates before any assembly
- Identity sheet/key visual approved and reused consistently.
- Each selected hero still passes face/hand/object/perspective review before animation.
- Short shot proof visibly contains its declared motion/light/environment effects.
- Internal motion reads before camera motion.
- No magic portal/gate imagery appears.
- No generic AI morphing, temporal face drift, waxy restoration, double exposure, or matte edges.
- Spatial shots use real/authored depth before claiming production 2.5D.
- Transition proofs are reviewed at actual duration, not accepted from metrics alone.

## Final-film QC later
Inspect actual exported media for black/damaged frames, freezes, repetition, loop seams, ghosting, missing/invisible effects, flicker/boiling, identity drift, source leakage, continuity, framing/aspect, runtime, and audio sync.
