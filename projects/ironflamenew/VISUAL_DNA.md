# IronFlameNew Visual DNA — LOCK V1 CLEAN

Derived only from the three fully extracted supplied references and the current WAV.

## Core visual world
An abstract human-to-presence transformation film: dark human silhouettes and faceted/crystalline bodies exist inside vast indigo/cyan energy fields. Warm amber/peach light behaves like a transferable inner core. Cool ribbons, contour lines and luminous rings visualize attention, contact and transformation.

## Palette
- near-black / graphite silhouette
- midnight indigo / deep blue
- cyan / aqua / cold white energy
- warm amber / peach / ivory contact light

## Form language
- human silhouettes and clean profiles
- large contour/linework intelligence or presence
- translucent warm humanlike presence
- faceted/crystalline dark human forms
- flowing ribbons and circular energy traces
- sparse circuit/constellation-like detail only when subordinate to the figure

## Motion hierarchy
1. quiet contact: extremely restrained proximity and glow transfer (REF-B)
2. directed light travel: one small luminous motif moves while the frame remains composed (REF-A)
3. transformation peaks: broad fluid ribbons/rings and larger spatial motion (REF-C)
4. camera motion remains secondary to internal motion

## Identity strategy
Do not invent a photoreal celebrity-like hero. Favor a consistent stylized adult human silhouette/faceted identity system so continuity can survive across generated stills, layered composites and living-image animation.

## Final-frame format
Original production media must be true widescreen 16:9 at 24 fps. The supplied portrait clips are references only; do not pad, mirror or blur them into the final frame.

## Hard exclusions
- no medieval/fantasy-warrior styling
- no wolves or unrelated animals
- no swords, armor, castles, forge-cliche props or runic-fantasy decoration
- no portal/gate imagery unless explicitly introduced by the user later
- no title/poster typography in production frames
- no generic AI morphing
- no slideshow/Ken-Burns-only treatment
- no unsupported NeRF/3DGS claims
