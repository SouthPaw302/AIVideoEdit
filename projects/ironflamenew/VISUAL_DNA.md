# IronFlameNew Visual DNA — LOCK V1

## Core world
**Mythic domestic surrealism.** A rain-soaked old house and its interior become a memory-space where architecture, weather, dust, iron, human silhouettes, translucent presences, and cosmic contour fields coexist. The world stays emotionally grounded even when physics bends.

## Protagonist
One consistent adult woman is the IronFlame. Dark hair; simple charcoal / iron-toned clothing; no armor requirement, no warrior cosplay. Lock her identity before generating the hero-shot set. Silhouette, profile, hands and partial face can be used strategically, but identity-bearing closeups must remain consistent.

## Reference DNA extracted from the three supplied clips
- **REF-A:** dark human silhouette confronting a gigantic blue contour/cosmic face; a small warm-white/amber orb travels between them. Moderate internal motion.
- **REF-B:** black-profile human figure with warm translucent head/presence emerging from the hand and moving into intimate face contact. Very restrained camera motion; light/contact does the work.
- **REF-C:** faceted/crystalline dark face surrounded by strong flowing blue/cyan ribbons and a white circular energy trace. Highest motion density of the three.

The new film combines those grammars rather than copying frames: **contour intelligence + warm memory-light + crystalline structure + fluid ribbons**.

## Palette
- Midnight / iron blue
- Deep navy and near-black
- Cyan / electric blue contour light
- Smoke gray / wet stone
- Warm amber / ivory memory-light
- Dawn gold reserved for the final resolution

## Lighting
Low-key blue environment with localized motivated warm sources. Faces and hands can receive amber transfer light. Highlights must remain controlled; no generic neon wash.

## Motion language
1. Internal motion first: contour flow, translucent form breathing, ribbon advection, rain, dust, light travel, reflections.
2. Camera second: slow depth-aware drift, conservative push/orbit, no constant shake.
3. Warm orb/light can travel across surfaces or between people as a recurring memory motif, but it must not become a magic portal.
4. Strong fluid motion belongs at energy peaks; quiet sections use low-amplitude living motion.
5. Use real/authored depth for selected spatial hero shots before claiming 2.5D production depth.

## Hard exclusions
- No portal / magic gate / fantasy doorway imagery.
- No portrait clips padded, mirrored or blurred into widescreen.
- No generic AI morphing or identity drift.
- No slideshow / Ken Burns-only treatment.
- No invisible effects accepted as present.
- No constant whole-frame shake.
- No unsupported claims of NeRF or 3DGS.
- Do not import unrelated medieval swords, wolves, battles, runes, or forge clichés into this lyric-led version unless the story genuinely calls for them.

## Format lock
True widescreen 16:9, 24 fps. Working production target 1920x1080. Reference videos define motion/style only, not framing.
