# Silver Coin — lyrics

[Verse 1]
The morning steals the warmth away,
the miller counts his gold.
We carry sacks and wood and grain
until the night turns cold.
But when the final wagon rests
and fire fills the room,
I drink to every scrap of joy
and laugh away the gloom.

[Chorus]
Raise your mugs and stamp the beat,
tonight we will not mourn!
What the master takes by day
we take back before dawn.
Play, my fiddler, fast and loud,
until the floorboards shake.
Poor in coin, but rich in song,
while living hearts still wake!

[Verse 2]
A merchant spoke of finest silk
and swore his love was true.
But when his little cup ran dry,
his promise vanished too.
So I took his hat and silver coin
and left the man alone.
Who brings a barmaid lies and boasts
may thirst out in the cold!

[Chorus]
Raise your mugs and stamp the beat,
tonight we will not mourn!
What the master takes by day
we take back before dawn.
Play, my fiddler, fast and loud,

Poor in coin, but rich in song,

[Bridge]
The farmer curses, smith turns red,
the carter sings too loud.
The maid has hidden the landlord’s bread,
the farmhand kisses his love.
Tomorrow work will call again,
tomorrow debts are due.
But wine and songs belong tonight
to those with little coin.

[Final Chorus]
Raise your mugs and stamp the beat,
until the morning breaks!
Everything that makes life bitter
tonight we drive away.
Play, my fiddler, fast and loud,
until the rafters shake.
Poor in coin, but rich in song,
while living hearts still wake!
