## Creative identity

Silver Coin will be a narrative **living oil painting** in the canonical project style named **Living Pre-Raphaelite Folk Romanticism**.

The supplied reference clips establish the rendering surface and quality of motion: luminous oil brushwork, emerald-and-gold natural light, floral and folk-period detail, idealized but human faces, graceful performance, and a world whose hair, clothing, foliage, flowers, and atmosphere move without losing the painted surface.

## Narrative world recovered so far

The existing Silver Coin concept contains:

- muddy village, road, and wagon imagery
- warm firelit tavern interiors
- musicians and fiddler performance
- dancing crowds
- merchant/barmaid interaction
- the silver coin as a recurring object and transition motif

These elements will be mapped precisely to the song after lyrics and musical structure are decoded.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Use Living Pre-Raphaelite Folk Romanticism as the primary visual language.
2. The two reference clips define **style and motion**, not literal characters, plot, square framing, or final aspect ratio.
3. Final production is landscape 16:9 unless the user later changes delivery requirements.
4. Build living scenes with character and environmental motion; do not make a slideshow or rely on slow zooms.
5. Preserve consistent faces, clothing, props, architecture, coin design, lighting logic, and screen geography.
6. Motion must preserve painterly texture without boiling, face drift, hand morphing, or disappearing objects.
7. Select effects from `docs/VISUAL_STYLE_CATALOG.md` and record their project-specific use in `EFFECTS_PLAN.md`.
8. Update GitHub after every meaningful production phase and before a chat/agent handoff.
9. Do not invent canonical lyrics; recover and verify them before lyric-timed editing.
10. Keep Silver Coin isolated on `song/silver-coin` until intentionally merged.
