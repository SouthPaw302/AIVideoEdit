# Silver Coin V6 — Sample-Video-Derived Storyboard

**Visual source of truth:** the two user-supplied sample videos, especially sample A's opening blonde flower-crowned woodland woman.  
**Rule:** every frame must look like it belongs to the same painted film as those samples.

## Recurring protagonist lock

The woman is the continuity anchor throughout the film:

- young adult blonde woman
- very long softly waved golden hair
- natural flower crown: small pink, lavender, blue and white blossoms with green leaves
- soft rosy complexion, luminous but visibly painted skin
- gray-green / pale sage folk dress with embroidered red-gold neckline and cuffs; cream variation allowed only when motivated by lighting/scene
- small pendant/folk jewelry
- gentle, intelligent expression; capable of warmth, mischief and defiance
- botanical objects and flowers are tactile, not decorative particle effects

Do not drift into a different face, brunette, modern styling, fantasy armor, photorealism, anime, game CGI, or generic tavern concept art.

## World lock

The whole film is an extension of the samples' woodland:

- saturated emerald and moss greens
- shafts of warm natural light through dense trees
- wildflower whites/yellows/lavenders
- old European vernacular wood/stone architecture when the story enters the village
- interiors retain botanical/green-gold continuity through herbs, carved wood, flowers, garlands and warm candlelight
- visible oil/pigment softness and romantic Pre-Raphaelite composition
- faces and hands painted carefully; no rough AI-concept-art anatomy

## Storyboard

### 01 — The flower and the coin | 0:00–0:10
The sample-A woman stands in the woodland exactly in the established visual language, holding a small yellow wildflower. A worn silver coin hangs subtly at her neck or rests half-hidden in her palm. This shot introduces the entire film's face, palette and material language.

### 02 — Leaving the green world | 0:10–0:20
She turns toward a distant village path visible through trees. Her long hair and flower crown remain identical. Foreground flowers and leaves frame her rather than generic scenery.

### 03 — Work at day's end | 0:20–0:30
She reaches the village edge as workers finish carrying sacks, wood and grain. Crucially the composition remains her film: she is foreground/central, workers are supporting figures painted in the same luminous green-gold language rather than replacing her with unrelated male concept-art portraits.

### 04 — Final wagon / warm window | 0:30–0:39.3
Twilight. A wagon settles beside an old timber inn. She looks toward the amber windows. Cool woodland greens remain behind her while warm tavern gold begins ahead.

### 05 — First toast | 0:39.3–0:50
Inside the inn, she lifts a small metal/ceramic mug among laborers. Flower crown and embroidered dress remain visually legible. Candlelight warms the same painterly skin tones from the sample.

### 06 — She starts the beat | 0:50–1:02
She stamps the floor / claps the rhythm and draws the room into the chorus. Low viewpoint catches her skirt, flowers/garlands and wooden floor without turning the scene into generic rowdy tavern art.

### 07 — Fiddler in her world | 1:02–1:13
A fiddler plays beside her. The camera composition links his bow to her movement; she remains visible and recognizable. Instruments, hands and faces are carefully painted.

### 08 — Rich in song | 1:13–1:22.7
Wide communal dance around her. She is the visual center and emotional conductor, surrounded by green garlands, amber candlelight and flowers that visually connect the tavern to the woodland.

### 09 — The merchant enters | 1:22.7–1:31
A well-dressed merchant arrives. He is seen partly over her shoulder or across the table so the film never abandons her perspective. His clothes are richer but still painted in the same old-world style.

### 10 — Promise and empty cup | 1:31–1:38
Merchant speaks grandly while showing an empty cup. She watches with a knowing half-smile. Tight two-shot emphasizing expressions, embroidery, hands and metal cup.

### 11 — Silver coin | 1:38–1:43.7
Close-up: a worn silver coin catches warm light in her fingers while the merchant's hat enters the composition. Her face remains softly visible beyond the coin; object shot does not become detached fantasy graphics.

### 12 — Defiance | 1:43.7–1:55
She takes the merchant's hat and turns away laughing; the room erupts around her. This is playful agency, not violence. Same woman, same crown, same costume identity.

### 13 — Chorus returns | 1:55–2:07
Back into communal rhythm. She places the hat on a laughing worker or raises it overhead while everyone stamps the beat. The silver coin flashes only once as a motivated accent.

### 14 — Fiddle / flowers / fire | 2:07–2:18.2
Closer performance tableau: she dances beside the fiddler, candle/firelight and hanging herbs moving gently. Forest-green shadows prevent the interior from becoming orange/brown generic tavern imagery.

### 15 — Village lives | 2:18.2–2:34
Bridge passage follows her through connected tableaux: farmer, smith, carter, singer. She passes through or observes each rather than cutting to unrelated portrait cards. The visual world remains continuous.

### 16 — Quiet threshold | 2:34–2:48
She steps outside into blue-green night air, catching her breath. Warm tavern windows behind, moonlit foliage ahead. Silver coin in her palm reflects both warm and cool light.

### 17 — Before dawn | 2:48–3:03.3
She walks back along the woodland path. The flower crown is slightly loosened but recognizable. Mist, leaves and early dawn light echo the exact sample-video environment.

### 18 — Final chorus / living hearts | 3:03.3–3:18
Return or memory-like intercut to the celebration: she dances at the center, but now botanical garlands and dawn greens dominate the palette. Long hair, crown and embroidered dress are unmistakable.

### 19 — Poor in coin | 3:18–3:24
She looks at the silver coin, smiles, and places it among wildflowers / into a small stream / on an old stone rather than treating it as treasure. The human experience mattered more than money.

### 20 — Rich in song | 3:24–3:27.4
Final portrait in the woodland: same framing family as sample A. She turns slightly toward camera as petals/leaves move naturally. Fade/hold on the exact visual DNA with which the film began.

## Storyboard QC gates

Before animation, reject any frame that fails one of these:

1. Does the protagonist still look like the sample-video woman?
2. Does the image use the same luminous painterly surface rather than a generic AI illustration style?
3. Does woodland/botanical continuity remain visible even inside village/tavern scenes?
4. Are hands, eyes, hair, crown, embroidery, cups, fiddle and coin geometrically plausible?
5. Would this frame feel natural if inserted between frames extracted from the supplied sample videos?

Only storyboard frames that pass all five gates move into animation/effects production.
