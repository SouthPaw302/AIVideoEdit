# Silver Coin Alternate — Visual DNA

## Canon
**Living Pre-Raphaelite Folk Romanticism** is locked. Preserve the tactile painted surface, luminous Romantic/Pre-Raphaelite beauty, traditional folk materials and garments, emerald/cool exterior shadows, warm tavern gold, and the recurring silver-coin motif.

The later successful Silver Coin production established the **recurring blonde, flower-crowned woman and the eight accepted V8 hero paintings** as reusable Silver Coin visual canon. The alternate may re-edit and extend this world, but it must not casually replace that identity.

## World
A cold working-day road and folk village open into a crowded warm tavern. Mud, wagon wheels, wood, flowers, smoke, woven fabric, instruments, worn metal, candle/hearth light and the silver coin all belong to one painted reality.

## Motion
Internal motion precedes camera motion.

- intimate/verse passages: restrained living-painting behavior near reference B
- choruses/fiddle/dance: stronger but bounded activity approaching reference A
- protect face, hands, instruments, costume silhouette and environment topology
- no endless zoom, global wobble or duplicated still-frame motion

## Spatial treatment
Primary spatial treatment is **bounded 2.5D depth/parallax plus layered compositing**.

NeRF is not a required label for this alternate. Only claim NeRF if an actual trained radiance field and visible proof are produced under the current capability contract.

## Atmosphere and reactive behavior
- low end: hearth depth, room warmth, fog/smoke density
- mids: cloth, crowd, hair and painted-surface movement
- highs/transients: silver glints, bow accents, sparks, rain points, brief motivated light response
- sustained passages: smoke/mist/pigment diffusion

## Transition language
Prefer coin/reflection matches, thresholds, smoke, rain reflection, candle bloom and cold-to-warm pigment travel. Transitions must preserve geography and identity.

## Rejection criteria
Reject glossy game-CGI, anime/cartoon faces, photographic skin pasted into paintings, texture boiling, face drift, hand/instrument deformation, topology drift, random particles and zoom-only animation.
