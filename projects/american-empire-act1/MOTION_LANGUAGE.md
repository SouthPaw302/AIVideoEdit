# American Empire — Motion Language

Status: project-level production standard derived from accepted Scene 01.

## Reference
The accepted Scene 01 master is the motion-language baseline for all subsequent American Empire scenes.

Measured comparison against the rejected/problematic Scene 02 Assembly05 pass shows Scene 02 was approximately 5x more active in normal frame-to-frame visual change than Scene 01. Scene 01 therefore defines the target cadence, not Scene 02.

## Core rule
Movement should come from the world, character, weather, light, and layered depth before it comes from the camera.

## Camera
- Default camera state is locked or near-locked.
- No global shake, synthetic handheld, repeated push-pull, or oscillating crop travel.
- Camera moves only when narratively motivated and should be slow, bounded, and infrequent.
- Do not alternate A/B hero framings merely to manufacture motion.

## Living-scene motion
Preferred motion order:
1. character micro-action or pose change;
2. rain, cloud, haze, reflections, practical-light response;
3. foreground/background depth separation;
4. restrained 2.5D parallax;
5. camera motion only if still needed.

## Hero image pairs
- A/B pairs are valid only when face, body, room geometry, lens/framing, and lighting remain close enough to read as continuous physical movement.
- If the pair creates a visible composition jump, hold the stronger hero and animate local layers instead.
- Large pose/framing differences require a motivated cut or longer dissolve, not a fake movement interpolation.

## Transitions
- Prefer clean cuts, short motivated dissolves, or scene-driven wipes.
- Do not hide geometry drift with rapid transitions.
- Transition duration should serve the dramatic beat rather than a fixed template.

## Audio
- Score remains continuous unless the story explicitly calls for silence.
- Dialogue ducks and score returns must use smooth ramps; no hard gain steps.
- Near-silence is approached and exited gradually unless an intentional shock cut is authored.
- Picture cuts must not force music cuts.

## FX
- Storm behavior remains coherent and source-driven.
- Interior rain is window-constrained; rooms stay dry unless explicitly scripted.
- Lightning, reflections, wet response, and practical lights react coherently.
- FX should add internal motion without destabilizing the frame.

## Scene handoff
Each scene must visually and sonically feel like part of the same film. The terminal state of one scene is the continuity anchor for the next.

## QC gate
Before approval, compare each new scene against Scene 01 for:
- motion intensity and cadence;
- frequency of full-frame movement;
- shot-to-shot composition discontinuity;
- audio gain discontinuities;
- continuity of weather, lighting, character, and environment.

Any scene that feels substantially more restless than Scene 01 without a story reason fails the motion-language gate.
