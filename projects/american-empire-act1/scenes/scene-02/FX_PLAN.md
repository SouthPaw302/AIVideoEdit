# Scene 02 FX Plan — Coherent Storm System

Scene 02 inherits Scene 01 visual identity and uses the reusable `coherent_storm_system` preset as its weather/motion backbone.

## Continuity rule
Do not change Claire identity, wardrobe, apartment topology, window orientation, palette, ink/graphic-novel treatment, or city geography. The reference motion language changes how the scene moves, not what the world looks like.

## System behavior
Rain, wind, moving cloud mass, lightning, wet reflections, glass droplets, exposed foreground motion, and practical-light response are authored as one coupled storm state.

### S2-01 — aftermath hold
- near-locked camera
- exterior rain in 3 depth planes
- glass droplets independent from exterior rain
- subtle cloud advection
- Claire and room geometry protected

### S2-02 / S2-03 — device anomaly
- room stays dry
- local device light changes only at its physical source
- storm continues through window as background system
- small wind response only on plausibly exposed curtain/fabric

### S2-04 — synchronized room systems
- multiple local device responses may synchronize narratively
- no global cyan pulse or full-frame lighting effect
- storm remains independent but coherent outside

### S2-05 / S2-06 — Paris coordination
- strongest multiplane storm read
- near rain/foreground travel > midground > distant skyline/cloud mass
- organized city light changes remain readable beneath weather
- wet exterior surfaces respond to lightning and practical sources

### S2-07 / S2-08 — Daniel information turn
- score duck supported by calmer camera and reduced gust intensity
- glass/reflection motion continues subtly
- protect Claire face, hands, phone and ink contours

### S2-09 — prepared-transition proof
- brief authored gust may precede reveal
- lightning event may support reveal only if source-motivated
- sky flashes first, then coherent environmental response; never arbitrary full-frame white

### S2-10 — Claire moves
- Claire/action motion leads
- storm continues behind her as a living system
- foreground props move only from her action or plausible wind path
- no camera wobble used to manufacture urgency

## Forbidden
- rain inside the room
- whole-frame weather overlays
- global shake/wobble
- arbitrary light pulses
- unrelated surfaces moving together
- face/body/phone deformation
- generic zoom standing in for internal motion
- cyberpunk palette drift

## Proof requirement
Before full Scene 02 assembly, render a representative proof containing:
1. interior/window shot showing dry room + glass/exterior rain separation;
2. city shot showing clear depth-layered rain/cloud/wet response;
3. lightning/gust event with coherent source-coupled response;
4. Claire action shot with protected geometry and independent foreground/background motion.

User acceptance of that proof is required before full FX lock.
