# Scene 02 — Storyboard Draft

Status: narrative/staging draft only. Score timing is intentionally unlocked until the current user supplies the Scene 02 score.

## Continuity entry
Scene 02 begins immediately after Scene 01. Claire remains in the same Paris apartment, same wardrobe and physical state, same pre-dawn weather, same apartment/window geography and same graphic-novel picture language. The emotional state carries forward: disbelief has become alert fear, but she is not yet panicked.

The scene must not replay Scene 01 compositions simply for convenience. It should expand the world while preserving spatial continuity.

## Dramatic function
Scene 01 told Claire that another government had been waiting. Scene 02 gives her the first observable evidence that Daniel was telling the truth.

The progression is: private warning -> anomalous systems behavior -> city-scale coordination -> Claire realizes the event is already underway.

## Provisional shot architecture

### S2-01 — Aftermath hold
Continue from the final Scene 01 window position. The room is nearly still. Claire lowers the phone slightly but does not disconnect. Rain and glass reflections remain alive. The score, when supplied, should be allowed to establish its own opening before any major visual escalation.

### S2-02 — The room changes first
A practical device in the apartment changes state without Claire touching it: display, router/status light, television standby, or another established electronic source. Keep the event restrained and believable. No floating holograms and no redesign of the apartment.

### S2-03 — Claire notices
Move to a source-derived profile/detail framing. Claire's face remains canonical. Her attention shifts from Daniel's call toward the unexplained device behavior. Internal environmental motion precedes camera motion.

### S2-04 — Network interruption
Multiple ordinary screens or indicators begin synchronizing. The important idea is not spectacle but coordination: separate civilian systems behave as though they have received the same command. Muted cyan technology may become slightly more prominent but must remain inside the established palette.

### S2-05 — Paris outside
Return through the same window orientation established in Scene 01. Distant buildings begin showing synchronized changes: lights switching in organized bands, signage/screens blanking or changing together, infrastructure entering a controlled state. Rain remains exterior and geography remains stable.

### S2-06 — City-scale pattern
Use practical multiplane depth across foreground architecture, middle facades and distant skyline. The city response becomes legible as a pattern rather than random outages. Avoid a generic blackout; selected systems remain powered, implying control rather than collapse.

### S2-07 — Daniel / information turn
Return to Claire and the phone. This is the scene's main dialogue/information slot, but exact wording remains unlocked until the Scene 02 script pass. Daniel's function is to tell Claire that what she is seeing is not an emergency response being improvised now; it is a pre-existing transition system activating.

### S2-08 — Proof
Claire sees a single unmistakable visual confirmation outside or on an ordinary screen: a coordinated civil/government channel, symbol, emergency protocol, or system status that could only have been prepared beforehand. Keep it grounded and diegetic; no exposition card should replace the visual evidence.

### S2-09 — Claire makes a choice
For the first time Claire acts rather than only receives information. She moves away from the window/bed toward getting dressed, gathering something essential, or following Daniel's instruction. Preserve wardrobe continuity until the action itself motivates a change.

### S2-10 — Exit beat
End on forward motion rather than another static revelation. The apartment remains visually continuous behind her while the outside city continues its controlled transition. This prepares Scene 03 to leave the room or widen the scope without making Scene 02 feel like a second version of Scene 01.

## FX continuity
- practical multiplane 2.5D from real semantic layers;
- foreground/internal movement before camera movement;
- rain only outside/on glass;
- device illumination tied to visible sources;
- no whole-frame wobble, generic zoom, arbitrary shake or global pulse;
- preserve Claire face, hands, anatomy and established ink contours;
- city/system changes should read as controlled coordination, not random cyberpunk spectacle.
