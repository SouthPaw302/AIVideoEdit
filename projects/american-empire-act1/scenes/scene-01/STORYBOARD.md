# Scene 01 — 5:47 AM, Paris

Target runtime: ~80 seconds
Production mode: cinematic-led hybrid
Hero frames: 6

## Story beat
Claire Delacroix wakes before dawn in a rain-soaked Paris apartment to a call from Daniel, an American source. He warns her that the continuity system she dismissed as a conspiracy is real. The final line reframes the coming collapse: the hidden government was not built to survive the system; it was waiting to replace it.

## Hero 01 — Paris before dawn
Wide exterior. Wet zinc rooftops, pale stone facades, rain, distant muted cyan infrastructure glow, warm isolated apartment windows. French noir mood but not oppressive. Camera language: slow push through rain and layered foreground architecture.

## Hero 02 — The ringing phone
Interior bedroom. Claire asleep in a restrained, lived-in apartment. Warm practical lamp remnants, cool window light, phone glowing on a bedside table. Composition designed for layered parallax: phone foreground, Claire midground, window/background city depth.

## Hero 03 — Claire answers
Medium close shot. Claire sits up in darkness, phone at ear, hair imperfect, alert but not melodramatic. Rain-streaked window behind her. Screen/reflection details stay subtle and plausible.

Dialogue:
CLAIRE: Daniel?

## Hero 04 — The warning
Profile close-up with layered foreground obstruction, perhaps curtain edge or glass reflection. Claire listening more than speaking. The city is soft behind her. Emotional turn occurs here.

DANIEL: You remember what I told you about continuity government?
CLAIRE: That conspiracy theory?
DANIEL: I was wrong.
CLAIRE: About what?

## Hero 05 — Lightning / city reveal
Claire at the window in silhouette or three-quarter profile as pale lightning briefly lifts the entire room. Paris beyond is elegant, rain-softened, with faint signs of hidden contemporary infrastructure but no overt sci-fi spectacle.

DANIEL: It's not the government that survives the collapse.

## Hero 06 — Final isolation
A strong final composition on Claire alone after the line dies. Reflection in the glass creates a second ghosted Claire while dawn barely begins beyond Paris. This image must support a very slow hold, tiny rain movement, breathing practical light, depth haze, and eventual cut to black.

DANIEL: It's the government that was waiting for it.

## Shot expansion from heroes
Each hero can yield 2–4 shots through alternate crops, 2.5D multiplane parallax, controlled digital dolly, environmental inserts, reflection layers, rain passes, shallow rack-focus simulations, and motivated detail cuts. The scene should feel shot, not slideshow-ed.

## Visual continuity rules
- Claire's face, hair, wardrobe, apartment architecture, window shape, city direction, and phone design remain consistent across all six heroes.
- Palette: pale gray-blue dawn, cream stone, muted cyan accents, warm amber practicals, restrained red only if naturally motivated.
- Avoid saturated neon cyberpunk.
- Avoid futuristic costumes.
- Paris must remain recognizable through architectural language, not postcard clichés.
- Image depth should be authored for foreground/midground/background separation so the repo's depth-parallax and living-scene effects can animate it cleanly.
