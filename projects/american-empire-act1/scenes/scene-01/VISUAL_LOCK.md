# Scene 01 Visual Lock

Format: cinematic comic book / graphic novel.
Audio: deferred until picture lock.

The six approved hero compositions are canonical source material for Scene 01. They must be animated and editorially derived, not regenerated or replaced.

## Image language
Use strong silhouettes, controlled ink linework, graphic shadow shapes, restrained halftone/print texture, pale gray-blue dawn, cream Paris stone, muted cyan technology and warm amber practical light. Avoid saturated neon and generic cyberpunk spectacle.

## Motion law
Motion is subordinate to the drawing and must be semantic:
- visible layered 2.5D foreground/midground/background separation;
- rain only outdoors or on/through explicit glass regions;
- independent reflection/glass behavior;
- subtle foreground material motion such as curtain/hair/object edges where plausible;
- depth-limited haze;
- practical/lightning illumination visibly coupled to its source;
- extremely restrained camera movement.

Protect faces, hands, anatomy, core silhouettes, architectural linework and panel readability.

## Rejected behavior
The previous FX pass is rejected because it showed rain inside the room, lacked clearly visible 2.5D parallax, lacked sufficient foreground/internal animation and did not convincingly animate lighting. Do not repeat or promote that pass.

## Current production order
`storyboard -> script -> shot packages -> representative FX proof -> user acceptance -> FX lock -> visual assembly -> picture lock -> score/audio -> final QC`
