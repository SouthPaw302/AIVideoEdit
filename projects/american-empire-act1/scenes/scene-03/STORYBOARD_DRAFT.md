# Scene 03 — Storyboard Draft

Status: pre-production storyboard. No production media has been generated from this draft. Timing remains unlocked until the Scene 03 score/audio structure is established. Scene 03 begins immediately from the terminal narrative state of Scene 02; this planning inheritance does not by itself promote any unapproved Scene 02 picture candidate to accepted master.

## Continuity entry
Scene 02 ends with Claire obeying Daniel's warning, moving away from the apartment window, gathering one approved essential, and committing to leave while Paris continues its controlled systems transition outside.

Scene 03 begins on that forward motion. Do not reset Claire at the window and do not replay the passive-revelation grammar of Scenes 01–02.

Locked carryover:
- Claire identity, facial structure, apparent age, body proportions and dark wavy hair;
- established apartment topology, window orientation and Paris geography;
- pre-dawn rain/storm state and coherent weather direction;
- graphic-novel/noir digital-illustration language;
- slate/pale gray-blue/cream palette, muted cyan technology, warm amber practicals, charcoal shadows;
- Scene 01 motion language: world/character/environment motion before camera motion;
- restrained standardized caption language;
- no saturated cyberpunk neon, floating UI, arbitrary holograms or style drift.

A motivated outer layer is permitted only as an on-screen departure action: a simple dark charcoal coat/rain layer over Claire's established apartment wardrobe. Hair, face, silhouette and underlying continuity remain stable.

## Dramatic function
Scene 01: something impossible is happening.
Scene 02: the systems are not failing; they are changing authority.
Scene 03: ordinary public infrastructure begins behaving as if the transition was anticipated and coordinated in advance.

Progression:
PRIVATE WARNING -> PHYSICAL DEPARTURE -> BUILDING-SCALE COORDINATION -> STREET-SCALE CONTROL -> CLAIRE MOVES INTO THE CHANGED CITY.

The city should feel controlled, not destroyed.

## Eight-primary-hero architecture

### S3-01 / H3-01 — Departure state
Immediate continuation from S2-10. Medium-wide apartment composition using the same room axis and established spatial relationships. Claire closes/secures the already-approved essential, keeps the phone, and commits to leaving.

Motion priority:
1. Claire's real hand/body action;
2. rain and cloud motion through the window;
3. wet reflections and practical-light response;
4. subtle local cloth/hair response;
5. camera remains locked.

Preferred behavior: `GENERATED_CONTINUATION` only for the actual close/lift/shoulder action; otherwise preserve the canonical plate.

### S3-02 / H3-02 — Threshold
Claire reaches the apartment door and opens it into the established building hallway. If outer clothing is introduced, it happens here as one motivated continuity event rather than an unexplained redesign.

The first building anomaly occurs before spectacle: corridor practicals and access indicators change in a synchronized sequence, followed by a physically believable series of lock/relay sounds farther down the hall.

Camera: fixed near the apartment threshold. Door geometry, hallway perspective and Claire's anatomy must remain stable.

### S3-03 / H3-03 — Hallway coordination
Long-axis hallway composition. Claire moves into the corridor while one or two anonymous residents cautiously appear at their own doors. No panic and no crowd scene.

Separate doors/access indicators change state in an ordered sequence. The visual idea is that unrelated apartments received the same instruction at the same time.

Movement comes from people, practical lights, door indicators, rain-muted exterior reflections at corridor windows, and depth—not camera shake.

### S3-04 / H3-04 — Elevator already knows
Claire enters the elevator. The panel/indicator activates and selects the lobby/ground route without a normal request, or overrides a user input with a prepared priority route. Keep the interface ordinary and diegetic.

Use animated indicator logic rather than new image generations for every floor state. Elevator hum, relay clicks and restrained cyan source spill carry the tension.

Camera: locked interior composition. No orbit, no lens change, no fake push-pull.

### S3-05 / H3-05 — Lobby handoff
Building lobby/entrance composition. Exterior rain is visible through glass; interior remains dry.

Access-control and exit hardware change state in a coordinated, mechanically plausible sequence. Doors/turnstiles unlock or route outward. A building/security terminal may show the same prepared transition motif first seen in Scene 02, but exact iconography/text must inherit the approved Scene 02 proof rather than invent a competing symbol.

Claire realizes the building itself is participating in the transition.

### S3-06 / H3-06 — Street threshold
Wide composition from the building threshold toward a rain-dark Paris street. Claire remains the foreground human anchor.

Street systems change in coordinated order:
- traffic signals synchronize;
- one corridor is deliberately cleared rather than randomly jammed;
- transit displays reroute or change status;
- vehicle movement slows/reorganizes rather than crashes;
- wet pavement reflects source-local signal changes.

Use real foreground/midground/background separation and restrained 2.5D parallax. Camera stays mostly anchored.

### S3-07 / H3-07 — Public proof
Over-shoulder or side-profile Claire composition facing an ordinary municipal/transit display and nearby civic/security activity.

The same prepared transition status/motif appears across otherwise independent systems. Distant police/security personnel receive matching instructions and calmly reposition barriers/traffic flow. No sirens-as-spectacle, tanks, invasion imagery or destruction.

This is the first unmistakable public consequence: authority has changed and infrastructure is behaving as though the handoff was preplanned.

### S3-08 / H3-08 — Forward into Paris
Long street composition. Claire moves with purpose away from the building, phone in hand, while the reorganized city continues behind and around her.

This is a true temporal-action beat. Use `GENERATED_CONTINUATION` for walking only if identity/world locks can be held; otherwise break the movement into a motivated cut between stable hero states rather than faking walking with pan/zoom.

End on movement, not another static revelation. Scene 04 should inherit Claire already outside and committed to motion.

## Alternate-state policy
Do not generate alternate images merely to manufacture camera movement.

Allowed matched states only where they represent real physical progression:
- H3-01 A/B: essential unsecured -> secured/carried;
- H3-02 A/B: apartment door closed -> physically opened into the same hallway geometry;
- H3-08: prefer true bounded temporal continuation; if unavailable, at most one matched destination state for a motivated cut.

All other hero compositions should remain single strong plates with internal living-scene motion and animated diegetic system events.

## Animated event strategy
Prefer temporal overlays/composites/GIF-style inserts for:
- elevator indicator progression;
- access-control/status lights;
- lobby lock/door state indicators;
- transit/public-information display changes;
- traffic-light synchronization;
- wet reflections responding to those sources.

These are scene events, not decorative overlays.

## Motion / camera law
Movement hierarchy:
1. character physical action;
2. rain/wind/haze;
3. source-driven lighting and reflections;
4. foreground/environmental objects;
5. depth/parallax;
6. camera movement only when the story still requires it.

Forbidden defaults:
- global wobble;
- fake handheld shake;
- endless zoom;
- repeated push-pull;
- oscillating A/B framing;
- architecture warping;
- Claire stretching/deformation;
- random city animation unrelated to a coherent system event.

## Weather / lighting continuity
The Scene 01–02 storm continues as one force system. Rain remains exterior/on glass. Lightning, if used, is source-driven and may illuminate sky -> glass -> room/building surfaces -> Claire -> reflections according to line of sight. No whole-frame white flash.

As Claire moves into the building, rain becomes mostly audible/reflected until lobby/street sightlines reintroduce direct exterior weather.

## Audio intent
Do not hard-cut the existing sound language at the scene boundary. Preserve a short atmospheric handoff from Scene 02 where available, then build with:
- rain and distant thunder;
- apartment/building room tone;
- lock/relay clicks;
- elevator motor/hum;
- restrained device/system cues;
- wet-street city ambience.

Do not lock final musical cut points until the Scene 03 score/audio structure is available. Any score duck/return must use smooth ramps.

## Caption intent
No new dialogue is locked by this storyboard. If Daniel/Claire dialogue is authored in the script pass, use the standardized compact lower-third dialogue window established in Scenes 01–02. Keep text off faces, hands, phone, door hardware, system indicators and major environmental events.

## Scene-end handoff
Terminal state for Scene 04:
- Claire is outside the apartment/building;
- she is moving with purpose rather than observing passively;
- the storm remains coherent;
- Paris remains functional but visibly under coordinated transition control;
- public systems now make the transfer of authority tangible;
- no conventional invasion or destruction has occurred.
