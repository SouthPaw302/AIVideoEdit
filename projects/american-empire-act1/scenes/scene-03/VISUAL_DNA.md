# Scene 03 Visual DNA

## Core style

Graphic-novel / noir digital illustration with bold inked value separation, restrained print/halftone texture and cinematic widescreen composition.

## Palette

- slate and pale gray-blue atmosphere
- cream Paris stone
- muted cyan diegetic technology
- warm amber practical lamps
- deep charcoal shadows
- rain-slick reflections

Avoid saturated cyberpunk neon, anime drift, glossy photorealism, floating UI and arbitrary holograms.

## Claire lock

Dark wavy hair, fair complexion, established facial structure, apparent age, body proportions and silhouette. Apartment departure begins in the established dark silk robe/peignoir. A simple dark charcoal outer coat/rain layer may be introduced only as an on-screen motivated action. Phone and approved essential remain continuous.

## Environment lock

Scene 03 begins in the established Paris apartment, then expands into a plausible building corridor, ordinary elevator, dry lobby and rainy Paris street. Door geometry, hallway vanishing points, elevator panel housings, lobby access hardware, municipal displays and traffic systems must remain ordinary and physically registered.

## Motion language

1. character action
2. weather and atmosphere
3. source-driven light and reflections
4. foreground/environmental objects
5. semantic depth and restrained parallax
6. camera only when still justified

Camera is locked or near-locked. True continuation is reserved for articulated physical actions. No global wobble, fake handheld, repeated push-pull, architecture warp or pan/zoom pretending to be walking.

## System-transition language

Authority change appears through ordinary infrastructure behaving in coordinated sequence: relays, locks, elevator routing, exit controls, traffic lights, transit displays and calm civic/security repositioning. Reuse the approved Scene 02 transition motif; do not invent a new logo or propaganda spectacle.

## Weather and lighting

The storm remains one coherent force system. Rain is exterior, on glass or visible through openings. Lightning is source-driven through sky, glass and surfaces; never a whole-frame white flash. Wet reflections respond only to actual local sources.
