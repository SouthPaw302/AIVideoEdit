Claire must remain the same person established in prior scenes: dark wavy hair, fair complexion, same facial structure, apparent age, body proportions, silhouette, phone, and approved essential. ### Environment lock

Preserve the established Paris apartment, door geometry, corridor vanishing points, ordinary elevator, dry lobby, exterior rainy Paris street, wrought-iron/Paris architectural language, skyline geography, screen direction, and storm force direction.

Infrastructure must remain ordinary and physically registered: real locks, relays, elevator controls, terminals, traffic signals, transit displays, barriers, practical lights, and wet reflective surfaces.

### Motion hierarchy

Use Scene 01 as the motion-language benchmark.

Priority order:
1. real character action or micro-action;
2. weather and atmosphere;
3. source-driven lighting/reflections;
4. foreground and environmental object movement;
5. semantic depth and restrained 2.5D parallax;
6. camera motion only when still justified.

Camera defaults to locked or near-locked. No fake handheld, global wobble, repeated push-pull, oscillating crops, endless zoom, or pan/zoom pretending to be walking.

### 2.5D architecture

When parallax is used, separate protected foreground, midground, and background planes from approved source media. Translate planes only within bounded vectors. Do not deform geometry, invent hidden surfaces, alter perspective, or change focal-length logic.

Rain, haze, window condensation, reflections, lights, vehicle flow, cloth/hair edges, and physical indicators must remain registered to the correct material and depth plane.

### Storm system

Treat the storm as one coherent environmental force:
- rain stays exterior/on glass unless a space is physically open;
- rain direction follows the same wind field;
- cloud/haze motion supports that wind field;
- lightning is source-driven through sky/glass/surfaces, never a whole-frame white flash;
- wet reflections react only to real local sources;
- indoor practical lights remain local and physically motivated.
