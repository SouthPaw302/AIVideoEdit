# Scene 01 — Suno Score Brief

Target: instrumental score for the existing ~80-second Scene 01 picture.

## Creative direction
French noir at 5:47 AM in rain-soaked Paris, restrained near-future tension, cinematic comic-book atmosphere. Elegant, ominous, intelligent, intimate — not bombastic and not overt cyberpunk.

Suggested musical language:
- ~68 BPM, 4/4, minor key;
- felt piano / sparse piano figures;
- low cello or bass clarinet;
- subdued analog sub pulse;
- brushed/percussive texture rather than full drums;
- very restrained muted synth texture and distant metallic ambience;
- optional subtle French harmonic color without accordion cliché;
- no vocals, no lyrics, no choir;
- no trailer braams, heroic rise, EDM drop, or oversized percussion.

## Picture structure
- 0:00–0:12 — quiet foreboding; Paris before dawn, rain, isolated warm windows.
- 0:12–0:36 — subtle pulse enters; ringing phone / Claire wakes; tension rises without becoming busy.
- 0:36–0:52 — conversation becomes unsettling; darker harmonic pressure and low sustained texture.
- 0:52–1:00 — one restrained light/revelation accent for the lightning/city reveal; not a giant impact.
- 1:00–1:20 — strip back after the reveal; cold sustained aftermath, unresolved final chord or tone suitable for cutting into Scene 02.

## Production requirements
- Aim for 80–90 seconds so the edit can trim head/tail while preserving the four dramatic sections.
- Keep dynamics controlled under future captions/dialogue.
- Instrumental only.
- End unresolved rather than with a finished-song cadence.
- Deliver WAV if available; MP3 is acceptable for the first timing pass.

The score will be analyzed and mapped into MUSIC_ANALYSIS.json only after the actual audio file is received. Do not invent BPM/section evidence before ingest.
