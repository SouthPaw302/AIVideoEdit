# Visual DNA — American Empire Act I / Scene 01

## Core look
Cinematic comic book / graphic novel. The image must first read as authored sequential art, not as a photoreal frame with filters.

## Palette
- pale gray-blue pre-dawn ambient light;
- cream and limestone Paris architecture;
- muted cyan contemporary infrastructure accents;
- warm amber practical lights;
- restrained red only when naturally motivated;
- no saturated neon cyberpunk wash.

## Surface
- bold but controlled ink/value separation;
- restrained halftone/print texture;
- stable linework and facial anatomy;
- wet-surface speculars and rain-softened distance;
- cinematic depth without plastic AI smoothing.

## Continuity locks
Claire's face, hair, wardrobe, apartment architecture, window shape, city direction and phone design remain consistent. The six canonical hero panels are not disposable generation prompts.

## Motion language
Motion belongs to semantic scene regions before it belongs to the camera:
- foreground curtain/hair/object movement where physically plausible;
- rain only outdoors or on/through glass regions;
- reflections move independently from dry surfaces;
- haze lives in distant/depth regions;
- practical lights breathe locally and affect nearby surfaces;
- 2.5D must produce visible relative foreground/midground/background displacement;
- camera movement stays slow and subordinate.

## Prohibited failure modes
- rain floating inside the room;
- whole-frame wobble or generic zoom used as animation;
- depth/parallax that is numerically present but visually unreadable;
- lighting changes with no visible source relationship;
- face/hands/ink lines warped by broad displacement;
- replacing the canonical heroes with newly generated variants.
