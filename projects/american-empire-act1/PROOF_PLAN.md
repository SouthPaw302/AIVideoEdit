# Scene 01 Representative FX Proof Plan

Do not render the full 80-second scene before this proof is accepted.

## Proof A — exterior depth/weather
Source: H01 / S01.
Goal: prove readable foreground-midground-background 2.5D using existing `FX2-SPATIAL-001`, exterior-only `FX2-ATM-002`, depth-limited atmosphere and plausible wet-surface response.
Pass condition: near architecture travels visibly more than distant skyline without global shake; rain is exterior and composition remains stable.

## Proof B — dry interior / window weather / practical light
Source: H02 / S03.
Goal: prove phone foreground, Claire midground and window/background separation; rain/glass motion exists only at the window/outside; practical phone/lamp light breathes locally.
Pass condition: walls, bedding, Claire and furniture remain dry; light changes remain source-local; Claire/phone/ink geometry remains stable.

## Proof C — foreground obstruction / reflection separation
Source: H04 / S07 and H06 / S11.
Goal: foreground obstruction has independent localized motion while Claire remains protected; glass reflection and distant haze move independently without deforming identity.
Pass condition: internal motion is clearly visible before camera motion; reflection reads as glass behavior rather than body warp.

## Proof D — lightning event
Source: H05 / S09.
Goal: existing light tools create a motivated flash/decay across room and glass while preserving Claire and window geometry.
Pass condition: no shake, no full-frame arbitrary flicker, and the illumination reads as one coherent light event.

## Failure conditions
Reject the proof if any of the following occurs:
- rain on interior room/Claire/furniture pixels;
- foreground/background move together with no readable depth differential;
- camera drift supplies most of the apparent motion;
- practical/lightning response is global rather than source-coupled;
- face, hands, anatomy, phone, window geometry or ink contours visibly deform;
- claimed FX are not visibly present.
