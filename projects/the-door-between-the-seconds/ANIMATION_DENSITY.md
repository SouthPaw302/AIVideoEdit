# Animation Density Contract — project-specific

Current-user requirement: more frame iterations and more animated frames per second, approaching animation rather than a slideshow; create video inserts where useful.

Implementation target:
- final timeline: 24 fps unless later changed by user;
- active passages: several authored key visual states per second, then optical/intermediate-frame synthesis only where it preserves identity and motion continuity;
- close/quiet passages: continuous localized motion (breath, eyes, hair, lace/velvet, candle flame, rain, smoke, reflections) plus controlled camera/depth evolution;
- choruses/industrial hits: higher state-change density, more aggressive spatial transitions, impact frames, short conventional-video inserts, and rhythmic cut/motion accents;
- no multi-second frozen stills masquerading as animation;
- generated in-between frames must materially advance pose, environment, camera, or effects rather than duplicate the same image.
