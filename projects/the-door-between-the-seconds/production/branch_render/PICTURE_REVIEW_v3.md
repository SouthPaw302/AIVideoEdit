## Director review
Status: **REPASS REQUIRED — artistic quality**

The candidate is a valid timing/motion/QC checkpoint, not the promoted picture. The procedural source bank keeps the 34-shot narrative and effect grammar alive, but the central Pandora figure is too mask-like and diagrammatic for the intended immortal-vampire presence. Several sampled shots also converge on a centered arch/window composition, reducing the setting variety promised by the storyboard.

## Repass targets
1. Replace the bright mask-face treatment with shadow-led, elegant vampire staging: profile, back/three-quarter silhouette, partial pale face, hair and dress movement, hands, reflections, and occasional red-eye/fang cues rather than a repeated frontal mask.
2. Increase composition variety: off-axis framing, balcony/profile shots, close detail inserts, low cathedral angles, deep corridor vanishing points, wet-floor reflections, crypt stairs, rose/velvet chambers, doorway thresholds, and first-light geometry.
3. Preserve the 34-shot timing and the 6-second silent cold open.
4. Keep dense motion and canonical FX, but make movement scene-specific rather than applying the same spatial grammar everywhere.
5. Keep full-screen 16:9 and no letterboxing.
6. Do not promote to ASSEMBLED/FINAL_QC until the original soundtrack is muxed and the completed audiovisual render passes cinematic scan.
