# STORYBOARD — Pandora the Vampire

**Format:** 16:9 full screen, 30 fps. **Runtime:** 169.12 s = 6.0 s silent cold open + 163.12 s song.

**Media rule:** do not scrap the recovered media. The chamber/window portraits become Pandora's recurring memory-home motif. Existing corridor, castle entrance, cathedral, temporal echo, departure, dawn and crypt frames are assigned to lyric beats. New visual variety is created by reframing, layered reflections, corridor repetition, perspective/gravity transformations, rain/embers/petals, and differentiated camera motion.

## Act I — Pandora before sound (0:00–0:06)
1. Pandora in the moon-window chamber, no music. Rain and candles move; she barely does.
2. A subtle turn/eye shift announces awareness. Music begins only after her presence is established.

## Act II — The house remembers (0:06–0:50)
3. Rain-black castle/threshold establishes the larger house.
4. Inward-facing windows return us to the familiar chamber.
5. Doorway/footstep imagery marks Pandora's deliberate entry.
6. Books, dust, iron, roses and fire become memory objects.
7. Corridor repetition and reflection ghosts make the walls "pronounce" her silence.
8. Close portrait: the house almost knows Pandora's name.

## Act III — Sleeping distance (0:50–1:16)
9. Cathedral scale replaces chamber scale.
10. Moonlit sanctuary stretches into impossible depth.
11. Familiar window imagery returns as the night she must not force to remain.
12. Temporal doubles embody "all our leaving."
13. First pale dawn enters the cathedral of roses: the unmade day.

## Act IV — Gravity and the hallway (1:16–1:56.02)
14. Quiet thunder pulses above Pandora.
15. The room tilts/inverts while Pandora remains the anchor.
16. Repeated corridor creates the endless hallway.
17. Reverse drift makes progress impossible.
18. Pandora reaches; reflected doubles close the missing distance.
19. Temporal echoes hold the second before contact.
20. Intimate candle/rain imagery resolves the "strangers become friends" moment without literalizing it.

## Act V — The passage opens (1:56.02–2:24.02)
21. Cathedral returns with harder musical impact and stronger motion.
22. Sanctuary depth folds toward Pandora.
23. Vampiric portrait identifies her fully: Pandora owns the night but chooses not to remain trapped in it.
24. Departure imagery becomes forward movement.
25. Dawn expands: the unmade day becomes possible.

## Act VI — Underground morning (2:24.02–2:49.12)
26. Pandora descends into the crypt in morning light.
27. Dawn enters without sound.
28. Roses and crypt imagery reveal preserved memory, not burial.
29. Final Pandora portrait slows to near-stillness under first light. Fade to black only after "underground."

## Cinematic continuity
- Pandora must remain visually identifiable across all shots: pale adult vampire woman, dark/burgundy gothic wardrobe, antique metal ornament, black lace, candle/moon/rain lighting.
- Chamber/window imagery intentionally recurs at the cold open, Verse 1, both choruses and the almost-touch. Recurrence is motif, not padding.
- Every return must change camera direction, layering, reflection behavior, light state or narrative meaning.
- Chorus scale must be visibly larger than verse scale.
- Verse 2 must contain the strongest spatial impossibility.
- Outro motion must decelerate rather than simply stop.
