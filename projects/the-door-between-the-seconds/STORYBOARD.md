## Story progression

Pandora enters a temporal house to recover the last human morning she never lived. The film begins inside an intimate memory-home, expands into sleeping cathedral distance, reveals a temporal/reflection version of Pandora rather than a separate second lead, then descends to a crypt where the unmade dawn becomes inhabitable underground.
