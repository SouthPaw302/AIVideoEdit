# The Door Between the Seconds — Working Script

Six macro sequences cover every frame; `RENDER_PLAN.json` records the 27 editorial sub-scenes. The exact six supplied stills are authorized for final-picture reuse and are animated directly alongside generated continuation/story frames.

## INTRO — frames 0–239
The woman approaches the inward-looking house beneath the rain.
- Lyric cue: There is a house beneath the rain / Where every window faces in
- Visual/motion: rainy castle approach; continuous rain, camera drift and reflective shimmer
- Transition language: dissolve

## VERSE_1 — frames 240–1319
She leaves deliberate footprints at the threshold; dust, iron and walls begin answering her presence.
- Lyric cue: footsteps / dust remembers / iron remembers flame / walls pronounce my silence
- Visual/motion: window, doorway, footprints, dust-and-iron chamber; 30fps camera/atmosphere motion
- Transition language: pigment

## CHORUS_1 — frames 1320–2099
The sleeping distance is revealed as an endless cathedral-city with a seam of unmade dawn.
- Lyric cue: Do not wake the sleeping distance / There is still an unmade day
- Visual/motion: endless arches, hard impact cuts, stronger parallax and audio-reactive accents
- Transition language: cut/pigment

## VERSE_2 — frames 2100–3300
The hallway has no end; she stops trying to cross it and reaches toward the moment before strangers become friends.
- Lyric cue: quiet thunder / hallway had no end / touched the moment before strangers become friends
- Visual/motion: corridor repeats, threshold echoes, mirrors and reaching gesture
- Transition language: pigment/cut

## CHORUS_2 — frames 3301–4140
Contact folds the distance and the house opens into a city suspended between moon and dawn.
- Lyric cue: repeat chorus
- Visual/motion: rain-black exterior, footprints-as-path, cathedral-city expansion
- Transition language: cut/pigment

## OUTRO — frames 4141–4893
She descends into the underground sanctuary; silent morning enters without conquering the dark.
- Lyric cue: If the morning finds this house / We were never lost / We were underground
- Visual/motion: crypt, roses, dying candles, slow dawn shafts, reduced motion into final stillness
- Transition language: pigment
