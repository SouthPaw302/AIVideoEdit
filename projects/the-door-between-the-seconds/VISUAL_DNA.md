# Visual DNA — clean rebuild

## Picture language
Gothic temporal realism: black and deep burgundy interiors, candle-gold practical light, rain-blue exteriors, wet stone, tarnished iron, red roses/fabric, aged mirrors and monumental pointed architecture. The world begins intimate and tactile, expands into impossible cathedral distance, then resolves in an underground dawn.

## Pandora continuity
Pandora is the single stable human figure. Preserve the supplied face, dark hair, pale skin, black/burgundy wardrobe and aristocratic vampire presence. No random costume redesigns, facial regeneration, body drift or unrelated substitute characters. The apparent second Pandora in verse two is a temporal/reflection construction from the same identity, not a new lead.

## Spatial grammar
- chamber/window = memory-home, intimacy, observation
- threshold/boots = physical anchor and commitment
- object macro shots = the house remembering
- mirror/reflection = temporal disagreement
- corridor/cathedral = sleeping distance made visible
- crypt = buried time / sanctuary
- dawn = pale spatial intrusion, never cheerful golden sunrise

## Motion grammar
Internal motion first: candle flame, rain, glass streaks, reflections, cloth, hair, dust, embers, petals, haze and localized light response. Camera movement is deliberate and music-directed. Verse movement is restrained; choruses widen and travel harder; verse-two gravity failure is achieved through independent region motion and perspective changes rather than whole-frame shake.

## Transition grammar
Use motivated transitions: doorway depth gates, reflection handoffs, red-fabric match cuts, light-peak handoffs, pigment gates and short dissolves. Avoid repeated generic crossfades and model-morph transitions.

## Color arc
1. cold open: black/burgundy + candle gold
2. first verse: rain blue enters the warm chamber
3. first chorus: deeper blacks, silver-blue distance, small pale dawn seam
4. second verse: desaturated/cooler temporal instability with red identity anchors
5. second chorus: strongest red/black/blue contrast and widening pale light
6. outro: stone gray, extinguishing amber, restrained subterranean dawn
