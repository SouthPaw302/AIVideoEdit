# Locked Shot Map — A Thousand Doors

The time ranges are structural guides tied to the 180.64 s master and may be frame-trimmed during assembly.

1. **S01 / 0:00–0:16 — Door in Nothing**  
   Black landscape; one freestanding door; restrained practical light breath; slow physical approach.

2. **S02 / 0:16–0:32 — First Crossing**  
   Door cracks open; first use of Threshold Prism Bleed; camera passes through rather than cutting.

3. **S03 / 0:32–0:50 — Infinite Corridor**  
   Deep repeating corridor with mismatched doors, atmospheric dust, localized moving light.

4. **S04 / 0:50–1:08 — Water Life**  
   One threshold contains an impossible flooded room / black water reflection; transition exits through reflected geometry.

5. **S05 / 1:08–1:27 — Forest / Childhood Life**  
   Warm remembered domestic fragments entangled with roots and forest depth; doors open into incompatible scales.

6. **S06 / 1:27–1:47 — Burning Future**  
   A future city / furnace horizon appears beyond a brass threshold; living flame and embers remain localized inside the alternate world.

7. **S07 / 1:47–2:03 — Hall of Selves**  
   Alternate versions of the traveler watch from mirrored or glass-backed doors; corridor geometry begins to fracture.

8. **S08 / 2:03–2:23 — Handleless Door**  
   Musical quiet zone. Corridor vanishes. One plain door and one distant figure. Minimal motion, almost no particles.

9. **S09 / 2:23–2:38 — Threshold Rupture**  
   Strongest Threshold Prism Bleed event. Doorframe light separates into spectral edge filaments and ghost-room exposures while maintaining the main composition.

10. **S10 / 2:38–2:52 — Inner Archive Storm**  
    Route 3 enters fully: doors, paper, memory fragments, silhouettes and room planes orbit around the traveler; audio-reactive controls intensify internal motion.

11. **S11 / 2:52–3:00.64 — Integration / Open Horizon**  
    Archive fragments collapse into one luminous plane; traveler crosses the final threshold; motion resolves rather than ending on a hard effect burst.
