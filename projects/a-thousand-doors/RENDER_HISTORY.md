## Accepted production path
The authored silent master was built as six contiguous 30-second-class production chunks, concatenated to 180.625 s, then married to the 180.640 s WAV and upscaled to 1280x720.

1. one first mux ended at 174.421 s rather than the complete master;
2. several held compositions triggered >2.5 s freeze detection.

Those failures were not accepted. The final motion revision added localized moving atmosphere, practical-light breathing, and a slow camera drift only through the forest/childhood memory shot.
