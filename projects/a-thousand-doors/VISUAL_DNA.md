# Visual DNA — A Thousand Doors

## World
An impossible architecture of thresholds. The film begins materially believable and grows increasingly metaphysical without losing dimensional coherence.

## Core geometry
- tall narrow doors, repeated but never identical
- converging corridor perspective
- frames within frames
- impossible negative space beyond thresholds
- late-film multiplication into archive lattices and orbiting door fragments

## Material language
- blackened wood, aged brass, stone, smoke, dust, dark glass
- restrained warm practical light against large cool shadow fields
- threshold light may fracture spectrally only when dramatically motivated

## Motion language
- camera advances through space rather than merely zooming a still
- environment motion remains localized: dust, smoke, light breathing, door movement, reflections, paper fragments
- thresholds cause spatial handoffs and compositional transformation
- final archive becomes more reactive and non-Euclidean as the arrangement peaks

## Character language
The traveler is a recurring human-scale silhouette/figure anchoring scale and choice. Identity should remain consistent within a shot family, but alternate-life glimpses may deliberately show divergent versions.

## Avoid
- generic slideshow pacing
- full-frame random warping
- indiscriminate particles
- literal claims of true diffraction simulation, NeRF, or 3DGS unless the actual underlying technology is used
- effect stacking that hides authored composition
