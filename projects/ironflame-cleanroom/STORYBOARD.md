## Continuity locks
- Same contour grammar, line-weight family and material response in all beats.
- No recognizable people, animals, architecture, fire, weapons, text, symbols or old IronFlame imagery.
- Internal material motion is primary; camera/parallax is secondary and bounded.
- Every render-critical value is a deterministic function of composition time and the committed canonical audiomap.
