# IronFlame Clean-room Visual DNA — Molten Cartography

## Identity
A completely new nonliteral visual system. The world is an abstract topographic field: contour lines, liquid-metal sheets, fault seams, moire interference and refractive pressure. It must never resolve into the people, houses, fantasy characters, armor, wolves, forge imagery, rain scenes or narrative compositions used by historical IronFlame productions.

## Palette
- near-black / obsidian field
- pearl-white contour lines
- mercury-silver reflective strata
- restrained ultraviolet and cold cyan interference only at high-energy events

No ember-orange, flame motifs or inherited IronFlame color coding.

## Composition
- predominantly orthographic or shallow-perspective fields
- stable world topology inside each section
- layered strata create depth without false free-camera 3D claims
- broad negative space is intentional
- no text except optional title/end card outside the song body

## Motion language
- slow tectonic drift on the base layer
- contour breathing tied to macro energy, not every beat
- transient events produce local line displacement and microglints
- measured roll clusters produce short ripple trains
- large surges alter stratum pressure or reveal a deeper layer
- drops simplify or remove layers
- final 233s drop begins irreversible visual subtraction to black

## Camera
Camera is secondary. Use restrained pan/push/parallax only. Most motion must occur inside the material field itself.

## Continuity locks
- no literal lyric illustration
- no recognizable human figure, animal, house, hallway, forge, weapon, armor or flame
- no historical IronFlame visual asset or prompt as input
- same contour grammar, line weight family and material response throughout
- deterministic seek: the same timestamp must yield the same frame
