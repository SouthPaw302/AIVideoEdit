# CASPIAN — THE DAY THE SILENCE SANG

Before the morning learned your name,
before the candles knew their flame,
the world was turning quietly,
keeping one small mystery.

Then somewhere underneath the sky
a door appeared where seconds hide.
No hand had touched it, no bell had rung—
and from the silence, you were sung.

Caspian, the stars leaned down to hear.
Caspian, another year is here.
Walk through the door, follow the light—
there are worlds that only open when you arrive.

The river remembers every stone.
The moon remembers being alone.
Trees keep stories beneath their rings,
and children remember impossible things.

So keep a pocket full of wonder,
keep your questions, keep your thunder.
There are maps no grown-up sees
drawn between the moments and the trees.

Caspian, the stars leaned down to hear.
Caspian, another year is here.
Run toward the strange, laugh at the night—
there are worlds that only open when you arrive.

And someday when the years move fast,
and birthdays turn to photographs,
listen closely between each sound—
the boy you are is still around.

The candles flicker.
The room grows still.

For just one second, time forgets to move.

And somewhere, behind the world—

the silence sings:

Caspian.

Happy birthday.
