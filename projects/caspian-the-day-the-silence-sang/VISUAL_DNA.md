# Visual DNA — CASPIAN: THE DAY THE SILENCE SANG

## Core feeling
Childhood wonder treated seriously: intimate, cinematic, mysterious, warm, occasionally enormous. Never a generic children's animation and never a music visualizer.

## Story-world rule
Ordinary reality comes first. The impossible grows out of real materials already present in the scene: water, bark, cloud, paper, paint, shadow, smoke, starlight, candlelight.

## Motion DNA from supplied reference
- near-locked or restrained camera while the image itself lives
- internal motion before camera motion
- identity/silhouette/eye anchors preserved during transformation
- progressive spatial replacement instead of crossfades
- scene material becomes the transition mechanism
- each metamorph has a legible start, middle and completed state
- transformations are local/coherent, not whole-frame hallucination

## Caspian identity
One recurring young-boy identity across all hero media. Wardrobe and face continuity are production-critical. Do not regenerate a different child per scene.

## Palette
Predawn blue-black, moon silver, river teal, forest greens, warm candle amber, restrained brass-gold magical fissures. Saturation may rise with wonder but must remain cinematic.

## Reggae/Dubstep motion translation
Primary environmental/body weight follows the natural ~76 BPM half-time feel. Faster subdivisions can drive fine particles, rain, root-light and small object behavior. Reggae offbeat may inform sway/anticipation. Dubstep accents may produce localized depth/material pressure. Never shake the whole frame on bass.

## Threshold rule
Do not repeat literal doors. Puddles, hollow trees, roots, photographs, walls, shadows and candlelight can all become thresholds.

## Anti-failure rules
- no procedural geometry as primary picture
- no repeated corridor motif
- no slideshow cards or floating memory rectangles
- no full-frame visualizer pulses
- no generic pan/zoom pretending to be animation
- no transformation that loses Caspian identity without explicit narrative intent
