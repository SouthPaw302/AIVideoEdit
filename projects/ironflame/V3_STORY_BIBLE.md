# IronFlame V3 — Story Bible

## Authority

Lyrics are the story authority. REF-A / REF-B / REF-C are the visual-language authority. The supplied vertical clips are reference/motion DNA, not timeline filler. V3 must be authored as true HD widescreen media built from enhanced/outpainted frames and animated internal elements.

## Narrative spine

Encounter -> recognition -> transformation -> separation -> reconnection -> integration -> resolution.

The "house" is not a literal building to be illustrated shot-for-shot. It is an interior symbolic space expressed through inward contour architecture, negative space, impossible orientation, faces, rings, ribbons, and light. No portal, magic gate, fantasy doorway vortex, tunnel, or unrelated scenery.

## Scene progression

### 1. House beneath rain
The silhouette discovers the immense blue face inside an inward-folding contour field. Rain is not literal weather; the sense of enclosure comes from flowing vertical/diagonal contour motion and low-density blue texture. The warm orb is present but distant.

### 2. Footsteps / doorway / darkness
The silhouette advances. The warm orb travels from hand-space toward the face/eye line. The transition into the next state happens through the orb and contour lines, not a portal.

### 3. Dust remembers / iron remembers flame
The smooth face resolves into the crystalline REF-C head. Internal ribbon flow appears. A ring forms from the same contour energy. The visual meaning is memory becoming structure.

### 4. Walls pronounce silence / name
The crystalline and smooth face states overlap and partially morph. Mouth/eye contours tighten, then release. The presence recognizes the silhouette.

### 5. Sleeping distance / night
The visual field opens into REF-B negative space. The warm translucent head hovers over the palm, farther away than before. This is emotional separation, not a new location.

### 6. Unmade day
The warm head begins changing into light. Its boundaries simplify toward an oval/ring state. Pale-blue values lift slightly, but the image remains abstract.

### 7. Quiet thunder
REF-C becomes the strongest kinetic passage. Ring pressure, ribbon flow, crystalline shimmer, and internal light respond visibly to the song without becoming a generic visualizer.

### 8. Floor forgets down / endless hallway
Orientation becomes ambiguous using the same faces/ribbons/contours. The field slowly rotates or reorients while faces remain anchors. No literal corridor or tunnel is required.

### 9. Reaching / strangers become friends
The silhouette reaches into the warm-head state. Light transfers from head to fingertip/orb. This is the emotional climax: first true contact.

### 10. Morning enters without sound
The contact persists while the palette lifts toward pale blue/cream. The orb becomes calmer and larger. Motion density falls instead of cutting to a new scenic idea.

### 11. We were never lost
Smooth and crystalline face states align. One ring/contour system contains both. The silhouette is small but no longer separated from the system.

### 12. We were underground
All three reference families resolve into one restrained final state. The orb settles in the palm. Ribbons slow. The ring dissolves. The final image reveals that the entire journey was inward rather than spatial travel.

## Transition grammar

Allowed transitions must emerge from existing image elements:
- orb handoff / orb travel
- contour-line continuation
- ribbon continuation
- ring grow / collapse / pass-through
- smooth-face <-> crystalline-face morph
- light transfer
- silhouette position match
- palette migration
- depth continuity
- object wipe using an existing face/ribbon/ring edge
- reflection or luminance match only when visually sourced from the frame

Forbidden transition ideas:
- magic gates
- portals
- doorway vortices
- fantasy tunnels
- generic lens-flare wipes
- random crossfades that do not carry a visual element
- unrelated particles/effects introduced only to hide a cut

## HD framing law

- Production master: 1920x1080 minimum, 16:9 landscape.
- No vertical clip placed inside mirrored, blurred, duplicated, or pillarboxed filler as a production composition.
- Source vertical frames must be enhanced/outpainted/recomposed into native widescreen hero media.
- The original source frame can remain compositionally important, but the widescreen extension must be authored as part of the same visual world.
- Review proxies may be smaller, but framing/composition must match the 16:9 master.

## Animated-still law

Every hero shot is a living scene, not a Ken Burns slideshow. At least two internal elements should animate when the source design permits it, e.g.:
- orb drift + glow breath
- face contour drift + eye/light pulse
- ribbon flow + ring deformation
- crystalline shimmer + localized light migration
- warm-head hover + internal opacity flow
- contour architecture drift + palette migration

Camera push/pan may support the shot but cannot be the only motion.

V2.2 is REJECTED ALPHA / technical assembly proof. It demonstrated runtime continuity and basic QC but failed story execution, transition design, HD-native framing, and animated-still richness. It must not be promoted to final.
