# IronFlame — Canon Lyrics

These are the lyrics supplied by the user for the canonical production.

There is a house beneath the rain,  
where every window faces in.  
I left my footsteps at the doorway  
so the dark would know I'd been.

Dust remembers every hand.  
Iron remembers flame.  
I heard the walls pronounce my silence  
like they almost knew my name.

Do not wake the sleeping distance.  
Do not ask the night to stay.  
Somewhere under all our leaving,  
there is still an unmade day.

Ah—  
the ceiling fills with quiet thunder.  
Ah—  
the floor forgets which way is down.

You were standing in the hallway,  
though the hallway had no end.  
I reached for you and touched the moment  
before strangers become friends.

If the morning finds this house,  
let it enter without sound.

We were never lost.  
We were underground.

Do not treat this as a demand for literal one-line-per-image illustration. Preserve the emotional and symbolic motifs while allowing the female IronFlame's mythic journey to embody them.
