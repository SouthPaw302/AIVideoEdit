# IronFlame — Visual DNA

## Identity

**She is the IronFlame.**

The protagonist is a recurring woman whose visual identity anchors the entire film.

## Emotional arc

`Haunted -> Determined -> Ascendant`

The film should feel like a myth emerging from the song, not a literal illustrated lyric sheet.

## World

A dark-fantasy / folk-mythic realm where the original lyric motifs become physical or symbolic spaces:
- rain-soaked house / threshold
- inward windows
- iron / forge / ember
- speaking walls
- sleeping distance
- endless hallway
- quiet thunder
- gravity inversion
- reaching another person
- underground chambers
- emergence into dawn

These can expand into roads, ruins, wolves, battlefield remnants, mountains, forge chambers, ritual spaces, or impossible architecture as long as the lyric DNA remains recognizable.

## Protagonist look

Target consistency:
- adult woman
- dark wavy or braided hair
- grounded, weathered beauty rather than glossy fantasy glamour
- soot / ash / scars / rain
- dark worn leather, iron, cloth, cloak
- practical, aged materials
- ember highlights and cool storm shadows

Avoid superhero sheen, pristine cosplay armor, or uncontrolled character drift.

## Palette

Primary journey:

1. **Ember Orange** — heat, memory, oath, forging
2. **Iron Blue** — rain, distance, solitude, night
3. **Ash Gray** — loss, underground, silence
4. **Dawn Gold** — release, recognition, continuation

## Texture

- forged metal
- wet stone
- old wood
- soot
- ash
- smoke
- rain
- fog
- ember sparks
- dust
- cloth
- oxidized iron

## Camera language

Mix rather than repeat:
- slow heroic push-ins
- lateral parallax drift
- low-angle grounded hero frames
- intimate close detail
- over-the-shoulder journey shots
- unstable/dutch motion in impossible spaces
- restrained handheld energy during conflict
- deep hallway pushes
- temporal locked-off transformation shots
- occasional abstract visualizer passages

## Motion density

Not constant.

Use quiet movement for haunted/intimate passages and stronger reactive movement around rises, impacts, and transformation.

Possible motion elements:
- breathing
- hair/cloak drift
- rain on exterior surfaces
- water trails on glass
- embers and sparks
- smoke/fog/ash
- moving practical light
- shadows
- falling dust
- reflected fire
- local architecture distortion
- gravity shift
- small character gestures

## Audio-reactive logic

Possible mappings:
- bass -> depth pressure / forge glow / fog density
- transients -> sparks / light accents / micro-impact
- mids -> smoke or ash movement
- highs -> ember/glint activity
- vocal sustain -> atmospheric bloom / focus changes
- instrumental passages -> integrated visualizer geometry

## Visualizer language

The user explicitly wants freedom to use old-school WMP-like visualizer ideas when appropriate.

For IronFlame, avoid a generic overlay. Integrate reactive abstraction into the world:
- glowing runic oscilloscope
- radial frequency ring around an iron sigil
- spectrum energy inside forge flame
- waveform trails in smoke
- particle tunnels between impossible spaces
- feedback/plasma transition during gravity inversion

## Recursive transitions

Preferred when they feel natural:
- raindrop -> ember
- ember -> sun
- doorway -> corridor
- iron railing -> tree branches
- smoke -> storm clouds
- reflection -> underground water
- fire -> lightning
- eye/glint -> distant moon or forge light

## Temporal painting opportunities

At least one substantial sequence should evolve over time rather than merely cut:
- storm gradually dying
- underground darkness becoming morning
- fire burning down
- architecture revealing its impossible geometry
- heroine moving from silhouette to recognized face

## Final feeling

Not “AI fantasy montage.”

It should feel like a singular mythic music film in which the protagonist, environment, animation, visualizer language, and song are all parts of one visual system.
