# Silver Coin — Visual DNA

## Style lock

**Primary visual language:** Living Pre-Raphaelite Folk Romanticism  
**Supporting language:** Cinematic living paintings  
**Spatial method:** Required hybrid Neural Radiance Field rendering. A compact trained CPU NeRF MLP maps 3D position and view direction to density/color for volumetric light and atmosphere; painted multi-plane scene geometry carries detailed subjects and architecture.  
**Motion method:** Natural character performance plus seamless environmental micro-loops  
**Transition motif:** The silver coin as reflective portal, match-cut object, and recurring visual anchor

## NeRF implementation boundary

The available runtime cannot run Nerfstudio/Instant-NGP or GPU photogrammetric reconstruction. The production therefore uses an actual compact neural radiance field for learned volumetric density/color and view-dependent atmosphere, combined with multi-plane projection for the detailed Living Pre-Raphaelite paintings.

Allowed claim: **hybrid neural-radiance-field spatial rendering**.

Do not claim: a full camera-captured photogrammetric NeRF, Instant-NGP reconstruction, or physically complete 3D geometry.

The NeRF must visibly contribute depth-dependent fog, light shafts, smoke, ember/rain volume, parallax occlusion, and camera travel rather than existing only as unused code.

## What the reference clips establish

- hand-painted oil/canvas surface remains visible at all times
- luminous late-Romantic and Pre-Raphaelite beauty
- idealized but emotionally human faces
- medieval/traditional folk garment shapes and embroidered detail
- abundant flowers, foliage, woodlands, and tactile natural material
- emerald forest shadows, warm gold light, cream cloth, floral lavender/pink, and small red accents
- gentle facial performance, head turns, gaze changes, walking/reaching/handling motions
- hair, sleeves, leaves, flowers, and petals moving in the same painted reality
- graceful, observant camera motion rather than frantic spectacle

The clips are **not** a command to copy the blonde woman, flower crown, forest scene, or square composition.

## Silver Coin world

A timeless folk village and roadside world opens into a crowded, warm tavern. Mud, wagon wheels, timber, candlelight, smoke, woven fabrics, instruments, worn metal, and the silver coin should all share the same painted materiality.

The world can feel historical and mythic without claiming strict documentary reconstruction.

## Palette journey

1. **Exterior:** rain slate, muddy umber, moss and forest emerald, cold silver highlights.
2. **Threshold:** warm window gold leaking into cool road tones.
3. **Tavern:** amber flame, honey wood, oxblood fabric, deep green shadow, smoky cream.
4. **Coin motif:** cool lunar silver against warm human light.
5. **Resolution:** softened dawn gold or candle-afterglow, chosen after the song ending is decoded.

## Texture and lighting

- visible oil brush texture and soft pigment transitions
- controlled luminous highlights rather than plastic specular CGI
- chiaroscuro shaped by windows, candles, hearths, lanterns, and moonlight
- rich color without neon oversaturation
- atmospheric depth through painted smoke, mist, rain, and dust
- no modern text, signage, logos, or accidental typography

## Character continuity

Expected recurring roles from the recovered concept include traveler/merchant, barmaid or tavern woman, fiddler/musicians, and the dancing crowd. Exact casting and story roles remain provisional until the lyrics are verified.

For every locked character preserve:

- face and age range
- hair shape/color
- costume silhouette, embroidery, and palette
- jewelry and carried objects
- handedness when playing instruments or exchanging the coin
- emotional arc and screen direction

## Camera language

- gentle lateral tracking through the village or tavern
- slow push toward faces, hands, instrument details, and the coin
- portrait-to-environment reframing
- layered foreground occlusion from doors, people, smoke, flowers, or hanging fabric
- motivated handheld energy only during crowded dancing
- no unmotivated orbiting or constant zoom

## Motion density

The target is clearly more animated than a slideshow:

- characters breathe, blink, turn, walk, reach, play, exchange, and dance
- hair and clothing respond to body motion and air
- tavern fire, candlelight, smoke, steam, and crowd silhouettes remain alive
- exterior rain, wheel movement, puddle reflection, mist, and foliage move selectively
- motion rests when the song rests; every pixel need not move continuously

## Audio-reactive behavior

- low end -> hearth depth, room warmth, shadow weight, and subtle camera push
- mids -> crowd/fabric movement and painted texture energy
- highs -> coin glints, instrument highlights, sparks, and rain points
- transients -> bow accents, footfalls, cuts, or brief light response
- vocals -> face focus, gaze, depth separation, and atmosphere
- sustained reverb -> smoke/mist expansion and painterly diffusion

## Transition language

- coin face becomes moon, window, eye, plate, or reflected tavern light
- coin spin or hand-to-hand pass motivates match cuts
- smoke, rain reflection, and candle bloom bridge locations
- selective temporal painting may turn cold exterior pigment into warm interior pigment
- transitions must preserve narrative geography rather than becoming random morph demonstrations

## Failure modes to reject

- generic glossy fantasy or video-game CGI
- anime/cartoon facial language
- photographic skin pasted onto painted clothing/backgrounds
- static zoom-only scenes
- texture boiling and painterly crawling
- face identity drift
- hand/finger/instrument deformation
- objects appearing or disappearing mid-motion
- random petals/particles indoors without story motivation
- square compositions merely expanded with empty side fill
