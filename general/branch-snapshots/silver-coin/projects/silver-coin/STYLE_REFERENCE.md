## Shared visual grammar

- Romantic/Pre-Raphaelite composition and folk-period detail
- visibly painted oil surface with luminous skin and cloth
- floral symbolism and nature integrated into costume/environment
- deep green shadow against golden hair/light
- natural, restrained acting inside an idealized painting
- environmental micro-motion unified with body motion
- no break into photoreal footage or glossy 3D

## Interpretation rule

Copy the **rendering language**, not the example subject. Silver Coin should use the same living painted quality for roads, mud, wagons, tavern architecture, musicians, dancing, firelight, smoke, human exchanges, and the coin.

The final video should be 16:9. The source clips' square format is only a reference-format artifact.
