## Global character lock

Use `assets/reference/ironflame-character-anchor.jpg` as the canonical identity reference. Preserve the exact recognizable adult woman: strong weathered face, high cheekbones, straight nose, intense dark eyes, dark wavy hair partly braided, soot/ash on realistic skin, subtle healed scar across the right cheek, worn blackened iron and leather, coarse charcoal cloak. Keep her grounded, practical, scarred, and human. Avoid glossy superhero armor, pristine cosplay, glamour styling, identity drift, text, borders, interfaces, logos, and watermarks.

## Anchor

Create a clean widescreen cinematic portrait in a dark ancient forge: waist-up, hands out of frame, ember light on one side and iron-blue storm shadow on the other. Cinematic realism with restrained dark-fantasy painterly texture, realistic pores, oxidized iron, worn cloth, smoke, and sparse embers. Expression: haunted but resolute.

## 01 — House beneath rain

Ancient isolated stone-and-timber house in a violent mountain rain; narrow windows appear to face inward toward a dim ember glow. IronFlame is a small solitary silhouette at the flooded threshold. Very wide low-angle establishing composition with foreground rain/puddles, house midground, fog mountains behind. Iron blue with one ember-orange interior pulse.

## 02 — Threshold

IronFlame crosses the ancient threshold from storm into darkness, intimate over-the-shoulder three-quarter view. Wet bootprints and rainwater cross dusty flagstones. Inside, inward-facing windows surround the same impossible central hall. Cold rain behind, weak ember light deep inside.

## 03 — Dust remembers

Medium-wide side view, face visible, authentic forge and tool geometry, strong ember light against iron-blue shadow.

## 04 — Walls pronounce silence

She turns toward a whispering wall while hairline ember cracks pulse through it. Long one-point perspective, wet floor, high inward windows, ash-gray/iron-blue palette.

## 05 — Road with wolves
