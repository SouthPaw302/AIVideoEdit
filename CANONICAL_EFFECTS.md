# AIVideoEdit — Canonical Effects Recovery Entrypoint

Before creating, coding, generating, or rendering a new loop/effect/transition for any song, read:

1. `general/reusable/README.md`
2. `general/reusable/CANONICAL_EFFECT_REGISTRY.md`
3. `general/reusable/CANONICAL_EFFECT_REGISTRY.json`
4. `general/reusable/PROJECT_TECHNIQUE_LINEAGE.md`
5. `general/reusable/EFFECT_PACKAGE_STANDARD.md`
6. `general/reusable/REUSABLE_EFFECTS_POLICY.md`

The registry currently preserves 101 named records mined from Silver Coin, IronFlame, Irish Eyes, Leave It by the Door, Sigh No More / Irish Eyes Spanish Hair, and the broader AI Video Production System specification.

## Mandatory rule

Search the canonical registry before inventing a new effect. Reuse/adapt the accumulated implementation whenever it fits the shot. Do not let useful work remain isolated inside a song branch, chat, local preview directory, or final render.

A canonical record does not automatically mean a technique was validated. Respect its status (`render_proven`, `final_lineage`, `rendered_project_unverified_per_effect`, `recovered_pattern`, `project_direction`, `experimental`, or `system_capability`).

Whenever a new reusable technique is created or recovered, update both registry files and preserve its implementation/proof/QC under `general/reusable/` when possible.
